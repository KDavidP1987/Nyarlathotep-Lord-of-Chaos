# DoD: Unit watch — stuck units recovered unseen, then removed, so a wave never hangs

**Size:** L. Several modules and a new game read (whether a unit fought), with new cfg keys, a new chat command and a new wire twin (api 7 → 8), no events.json or state.json change:
- **Logic:** new Logic/UnitWatch.cs (the standing and off-ground rules, the landing rule, the tries, the backstop, the lines, each pure behind a world interface as hunting-grounds' IRegionWorld), Limits (three IntLimit-style keys), EventAdmin (`unstick`, `event info`), CommandArgs, ApiLines/Wire (the twin's line, Api 8), Engine (no change to Due or Defeated: a removed unit leaves the ledger like any other).
- **Services:** SpawnTracker (the watch world: reads, the recovery move on our own units, the staged removal), EventScheduler (one tick phase), EventRuntime (the end paths), HealthMonitor (two entries), ApocalypseAction (a removed unit leaves the record's living counts), Config/Settings.cs ([UnitWatch]), Commands/EventCommands.cs and Commands/ApiAdminCommands.cs, and the fight read chosen by the spike (a guarded patch beside Patches/DeathEventPatch.cs, or a sample inside the tick).
- **Tools:** tools/vrclient/NyarDevTools (a dev-only strand command, never shipped), tools/vrclient/scenarios/unit-watch*.vrs, tools/preflight-checks.json, tools/paths-manifest.txt, tools/data-inventory.json.
No new external dependency.
**Planned:** by a drafting subagent for the orchestrating session on 2026-10-14, from design §9 D54 (the owner's decision of 2026-10-14, recorded below as validated assumptions), Epic A38 and the recon below; gaps the code and the decision do not settle are labelled `reversible` with their fallback, because the Halloween track runs unattended between owner sessions. The Halloween track's process applies (design §9 D34): one subagent review round, two at most; Codex once on the child's diff before the joint release; vrclient scenarios as the in-game evidence; the owner judges only look and feel and anything needing a second player; rubric 3, so the builder never passes a manual item.
**Request:** design §9 D54 (owner, 2026-10-14): "Before Halloween, its own child (unit-watch): a unit of ours that has not moved and has not fought for about 90 s, or stands where the walk check finds no walkable ground, is first moved back (hidden, never beside a player or inside claimed territory) to walkable ground near its group or the nearest player in scope; still stuck after 2 tries, it is removed and counts as gone for its wave, with no kill credit. A wave that waits to be cleared and sees no kill or hit for N minutes (default 10) clears its leftovers the same way, and `.nyar event unstick <id>` does it by hand. Applies to every event's units: waves, swarm, revenge, hunting-grounds and apocalypse hordes and roamers." The owner's problem: "it only would summon a new wave once all of the units were defeated. However, if one of those units gets stuck in a place where it can't be attacked or where the players can't reach it, then it's possible that they wouldn't be able to finish the wave. The wave would be stuck until the timer ran down … if there's a way to detect that something is stuck, either due to the teleporting process or due to the spawning process."

**North Star:** a server admin's wave events and apocalypse never hang on a unit no player can reach: such a unit is moved back unseen and, failing twice, removed with no kill credit so the next wave comes, inside the tick budget and the caps, with an admin command for it now, released with apocalypse as 0.13.0 before 2026-10-31; finished when D1-D33 have evidence.

## Purpose & typical use
A server admin runs wave events and the zombie apocalypse for Halloween. A waveList wave that waits for its clear (design §9 D40) spawns its next wave only when every unit of it is gone: Logic/SpawnLedger.cs WaveCleared (no waiting or in-flight order and no tracked unit of the wave), read by Logic/Engine.cs Due and Defeated. Such a wave has no timer to fall back on, so one unit that ends up where no player can reach it, after a hidden jump, a regroup, an arena or region return or a bad spawn spot, holds the event until its duration ends. The admin would say: "a wave never gets stuck on one spider on a rock."

Today nothing detects a stuck unit (recon: no stuck, idle or unreachable detection in Logic/ or Services/); the closest rules are lifetimes and DestroyWhenDisabled (Services/SpawnTracker.cs), the regroup after a spawn, swarm's arena return (Logic/Arena.cs), hunting-grounds' region lock and jump waits (Logic/HuntingGrounds.cs) and apocalypse's straggler hops (Logic/ApocalypseHost.cs). The job: find a stuck unit (D2, D3, D4), put it back unseen (D5), remove it after two tries with no kill credit so the wave moves on (D6, D7, D8), clear a quiet wave's leftovers (D9) and let an admin do it now (D12, D13). It extends every event kind and coexists with every mover of ours, which keep working unchanged (D22).

## Use cases
### Typical
The admin runs a waveList event whose wave 2 waits for wave 1's clear. A spider of wave 1 lands on a rock top after a regroup; the player below cannot reach it and it does not move or fight. It loses no health and its target stays beyond 5 m, so whatever fight signal the spike chose it has not fought; after 90 s holding the player as its target it is stuck (D2); the watch waits until no player is within 100 m of it, or at most 90 s (D6, S-10), and moves it unseen to walkable ground beside its group (D5). It fights there and is cleared. Another spider sinks under the ground after a jump; two checks 10 s apart find no ground, it is stuck at once (D3), recovered, sinks again, recovered, and after its second failed try it is removed with no kill credit (D6, D8); wave 2 comes within 10 s of its despawn (D7). The admin sees the counts in `.nyar event info` (D15).

### Minimal stretch
- An event with one unit that stands at its Point with no player near: never stuck (D2).
- Enabled false: nothing is marked or moved, and `unstick` refuses (D10, D12).
- StallMinutes 0: no backstop; the per-unit rules still run (D9).
- `unstick` on an event with every unit fighting: "0 units marked, n fighting left alone", nothing changes (D12).
- Used once: the rows die with the units and the event; nothing is written to disk (D11, D17).

### Maximal stretch
- Volume: at MaxTrackedUnits (default 150, at most 500) every unit is checked each 5 s from cheap reads; the walk reads are bounded at 120 a check across all events and at most 10 units are moved per check (D10, D5); removals go through MaxDespawnsPerTick (D6); D28 measures the tick with every rule running.
- Abuse: a player who stands beside an unreachable unit to keep it from being moved delays it at most StuckSeconds per try, then it is removed (D6, S-10); a player cannot make the watch move a unit into a castle (D5); a player cannot farm credit from removals (D8); a non-admin cannot run `unstick` (D12, D13).
- Repeated use: `unstick` twice marks the same units once (a marked unit is not marked again, D12); two events' units are watched each in their own instance (D2); an event stopped and restarted inside its grace never has the old instance's units moved or removed (D2, D5).

## Business rules
1. **Watched set** (D2, S-1): every live unit SpawnTracker tracks for a running event instance (its event id, spawned at or after the instance start; wave-sets A3), whatever the event kind: wave units (SpawnWaves: wave-sets, swarm, revenge, hunting-grounds) and apocalypse roamers and hordes. Units of `.nyar spawn` and of an earlier instance are never watched.
2. **Stuck by standing** (D2, S-4, S-5): no more than 5 m of movement and no fight for StuckSeconds (90 s), while the unit held a player as its aggro target (A2; an idle unit near a player is not stuck). A fight is a hit on or by a player (D4). A move by another mover of ours counts as movement.
3. **Stuck off the ground** (D3, S-6): no free and grounded walk-check tile at the unit's position at its level or a neighbouring one, on two checks at least 10 s apart; a fight in the last StuckSeconds overrides it; an unanswered read never marks.
4. **Recovery** (D5, S-7, S-10): only while no player is within 100 m of the unit; landing free and grounded, 50 m or more from every player, outside claimed territory, inside scope regions or the arena when the event has them; near its group (10 m), else 50-60 m from the nearest eligible player in scope, else near its anchor (10 m); at most 10 moves a check.
5. **Tries and removal** (D6, S-8, S-10): 2 tries; a try fails with no landing, with no unseen moment within StuckSeconds, or when the unit is stuck again within StuckSeconds after the move; a fight or (for a per-unit mark) no longer being stuck clears it; after 2 failed tries a staged despawn, waiting at most StuckSeconds for no player within 100 m.
6. **Gone, no credit** (D7, D8): a removed unit leaves the ledger as any despawn, so the wave's clear, the event's defeat and an apocalypse horde's count see it gone; nothing a death raises is raised.
7. **Stall backstop** (D9, S-13): waves whose clear the event waits on with no timer; quiet (no death, no fight) for StallMinutes (10) → every leftover marked "stall"; such a mark clears only by a fight.
8. **Precedence** (4.4): the kill switch (purge, pillar off) and Enabled=false beat the watch (D10); the caps and the despawn and walk budgets beat it (D10, D6); claimed territory, the hidden rules, the scope regions and the arena beat the landing choice, and a unit with no lawful landing fails its try rather than land unlawfully (D5); a fight beats both stuck rules (D2, D3); a player's kill beats a removal (a marked unit killed by a player keeps its credit, D8); the other movers of ours run first in the tick and the watch uses only the walk budget they left (D10). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
9. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every watched unit" is SpawnTracker's ledger filtered by event id and instance start, which includes apocalypse's rebuilt units after a resume (D2, D11); "every end path" is the nine of D11; "every consequence of a death" is the death patch's consumers, SpawnTracker.Died's scoreboard feed, DeathRule's VBloodKilled, the FactionKills and revenge heat counters, apocalypse's spread and the kill announcement, each a case of D8; "every mover of ours" whose move counts as movement is the regroup, swarm's arena return, hunting-grounds' region lock and jumps, and apocalypse's hops, rejoins and march (D2); "every reader of the new keys" is Config/Settings.cs, the watch phase, EventAdmin (`unstick`, `event info`) and the twin (D1, D10, D12, D13, D15); "every wave the backstop watches" is the three kinds of D9, and the units-form, growth and swarm waves are excluded because their interval timer always advances them (Logic/Growth.cs sets afterSeconds).
- whole: none — no list of parts adds up to a total; the counts in `event info` are tallies of separate events, each checked on its own by D15
- siblings: wave units, apocalypse roamers, apocalypse hordes

### States
- **7.1** Empty: an event with no watched unit does nothing and logs nothing (D16); first run: a unit's first window starts at its first check (D2); partial: a check cut off by the walk budget reads the rest at the next check (D10); error: a failing read or move holds its health entry (D18); a unit waiting for an unseen moment logs its waiting line once (D16).
- **7.2** Concurrent use: two events' units are watched each in their own instance, and the 10-move and 120-walk bounds are shared across them (D5, D10); the watch and another mover never both move a unit in one check, because the watch runs after the movers and a unit they moved has moved (D2); `unstick` arriving while the backstop fires marks each unit once (D12); a player killing a marked unit while its removal waits keeps the credit and the removal is dropped (D8); Claude and the build tooling act on the dev server only between sessions, wrapped by the snapshot (D32).

## Performance
- **13.1** Budget: the existing tick budget, 5 ms average at the default caps (automation D21). Recon measurement: apocalypse's Session 2 timing span (docs/dod/apocalypse.md Log, D29 pass) held 10/10 windows under 5 ms with 50 or more tracked and roamers jumping; a walk check costs about 0.4 ms in game (hunting-grounds A3, "slow tick: 392 ms (region 290 ms …)" at 736 checks). The watch's hot path is one position read and one fight lookup per watched unit every 5 s (cheap ECS reads, as the arena's "arena 0.1 ms" for 24 units, swarm Session 1), plus the walk reads, bounded at 120 a check (about 50 ms in one tick every 5 s, about 0.3 ms averaged over a 5 s window at 30 ticks a second), and at most 10 moves a check. The fight read's own cost is measured in D19 and the whole in D28.
- **13.2** Bounds: 120 walk checks a check across all events (S-11; the case is 150 tracked units each read off the ground in one tick, about 360 ms, and the excluded case is reading every unit's ground every check, so a unit's off-ground reading may come up to about 40 s later at the default cap); 10 moves a check (S-11; the excluded case is a whole wave recovered in one tick, which takes a few checks); removals through MaxDespawnsPerTick (default 5); MaxTrackedUnits bounds the watched set, as today.

## Out of scope
- **Considered and excluded (15.1):**
  - Replacing a unit that died before it engaged, and any per-event replacement budget: design §9 D53, a 1.0 child; this child only builds the fight read it will reuse (D4).
  - A per-event key (`unitWatch` in events.json) or a per-event StuckSeconds: global cfg keys only, so 0.12.0 never disables a definition (S-2).
  - Real pathing to bring a unit down from a ledge: the pathing study (design §9 D50, D55).
  - Watching native NPCs, or units of `.nyar spawn`: only event units we spawned (Business rules 1).
  - Moving players to reach a unit: only our own units are moved (CLAUDE.md › Spawn & buff safety).
  - A push or wire read for the watch's counts: only the twin; `event info` shows the counts (D13, D15).
  - Respawning a removed unit elsewhere: a removal is final for its wave (D7).
- **Deferred (15.2):**
  - Replacements (design §9 D53), scaling to the players (D52), ambush spacing at hemmed-in spots and the pathing study (D50): the 1.0 order of design §9 D55, each its own child after Halloween.
  - The 500-unit budget run: Epic D24 at the Epic's close.
