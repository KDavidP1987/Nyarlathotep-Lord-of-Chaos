4. Business rules & invariants

What the plan says under “Business rules”

  1. **One action kind.** `"type": "Apocalypse"` is a third action kind beside SpawnWaves and Empower; its definition runs under the Spawns pillar switch (S-6) and its own validation (D1). A definition carries exactly one action, as today.
  2. **Hotspots** (D2): found once per run at the start, from the faction's eligible natives in scope (cells of 100 m, minNatives, minSpacing, ranked by count), kept for the run and its resumes; read-only towards the natives.
  3. **Roamers** (D3): topped up to perHotspot at each hotspot at the start and at each horde rise, never above roamers.max; they go after the nearest eligible player anywhere on the map, moved by hunting-grounds' mover with no region bound (design §9 D49, A2).
  4. **Spread** (D4, design §9 D44): each qualifying kill, of a player or an NPC not ours, by a roamer or a horde unit queues perKill roamers at the victim's position, each joining the nearest hotspot, under roamers.max, perMinute in any 60 s and MaxTrackedUnits.
  5. **Hordes** (D5): horde k rises at start + firstAfterMinutes + (k−1) × everyMinutes at the next hotspot in turn with size + (k−1) × growth units under the caps; a horde that queued nothing has not risen and retries every 60 s.
  6. **March** (D6): the front moves at marchSpeed m/s along the straight line from its hotspot to the goal, holding while a player is within 60 m of it, except within goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, where nothing holds it; its units keep up with it (S-9).
  7. **Outcomes** (D8, design §9 D44): lost when a horde's front is within goal.radius with a living unit within goal.radius + 30 m of the goal; won when every horde has risen and none has a living unit or pending order; survived when the duration ends first. Lost beats won on one tick. An admin stop shows the scoreboard without an outcome; purge, pillar off and a fault cancel show nothing (D9).
  8. **Resume** (D12, design §9 D37, D45): only an apocalypse resumes, from its record, after the boot sweep, with units rebuilt fresh; its clock keeps running while the server is down and each horde missed while down rises once at the resume, in order; the resume waits for the boot sweep's drain up to 5 min, then proceeds with one warning; its resolved goal comes from the record; every other event is cancelled on boot (Epic S-11).
  9. **Lifetime** (D13): every apocalypse unit has a rolling LifeTime of at most 2 h plus the drain margin, renewed while the run lives.
  10. **Precedence** (4.4): the kill switch beats everything (a purge ends the run, removes its record and despawns staged); the caps beat the definition (a clamped horde is still that horde; a zero-queued one has not risen); a definition edited while running keeps the running instance on its old definition, and a resume reads the current one, refusing it when it changed kind or has fewer hordes than had risen (D12); validation beats chat (D16). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
  11. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every unit of the run" is the ledger's orders and units carrying its event id, from every spawn path (start top-up, rise top-up, spread, horde rise, horde retry, resume rebuild) and every removal path (died, despawned, LifeTime, removed by the game, failed spawn, end cleanup, purge), reconciled by D15's tally; "every end path" is won, lost, survived, stop, purge, pillar off, fault and restart (D8, D9, D12); "every name output" is the outputs PrivacyTests enumerates plus the record (D19); "every native write" is checked by D2's source scan over the hotspot reader.

D1 Apocalypse schema

a definition may carry `"action": { "type": "Apocalypse", … }` with `faction` (a Faction_* name the catalog knows), `units` 1-10 entries of `prefab`, `count` 1-50 (its weight in a pick), `chance` 0.05-1.0 and an optional `modifiers` object under the ParseModifiers rules, `hotspots { count 1-5, minNatives 3-50, minSpacing 100-3000 }`, `roamers { perHotspot 1-10, perKill 0-2, max 1-100, perMinute 1-30, huntRange 20-60 }`, `hordes { count 1-10, firstAfterMinutes 0-1440, everyMinutes 5-1440, size 5-50, growth 0-20 }`, `goal { location (Point with x, z and optional y, or Admin), radius 10-100, name 1-32 printable characters }`, `marchSpeed` 0.5-5.0 m/s, an optional `bosses` list (absent, or 1-3 entries of `prefab`, `count` 1-3 and optional `modifiers`, under revenge D28's rules), an optional `scope` and `scoreboard` (absent = true); for this kind `durationSeconds` is 600-604800 (7 days) while every other kind keeps 30-7200; an Admin goal needs a Manual trigger; a definition whose last horde would rise at or after the duration (firstAfterMinutes × 60 + (count − 1) × everyMinutes × 60 ≥ durationSeconds) is refused with "action.hordes: last horde rises at or after the duration"; an unknown key names its path ("unknown field action.<path>"); every refusal disables the definition with one reason that names the field and fits 120 bytes

D2 Hotspots found, natives untouched

at the start, Logic/Apocalypse.cs Hotspots.Pick groups the faction's eligible native NPCs (Logic/Empowerment.cs Eligibility rules: not a V Blood, not a prisoner or non-combatant, not a unit of ours) standing inside the action's scope into 100 m cells, keeps cells with at least minNatives, ranks them by count (ties by x, then z), takes up to `count` at least minSpacing apart, and puts each hotspot at the mean position of its cell's natives; fewer than one hotspot refuses the start with "no hotspot: fewer than <n> <faction> natives in one place in scope" and logs it; the read never writes, buffs, moves or destroys a native entity, checked by a source scan of the hotspot reader that fails on any EntityExtensions write or destroy call and reports "native scan: 0 files" over an empty set · twins: none — hotspots are found once per run, for every unit kind alike

D3 Roamers hunt map-wide

at the start and at each horde rise, every hotspot is topped up to `perHotspot` living roamers drawn from `units` by weight and chance, spawned within 15 m of the hotspot on walkable ground through SpawnTracker; live roamers never exceed `roamers.max`; a spread roamer (D4) counts towards its nearest hotspot's perHotspot at a top-up. Roamers are bound to no hotspot and no region (design §9 D49): each hotspot's roamers go after the eligible player nearest the mean position of those roamers, anywhere on the map (online, alive, a usable position, not in claimed territory, not in PvP combat; ties by key), moved by hunting-grounds' mover over our own units only (a hidden jump only while no online player stands within 100 m of the unit, landing at least 50 m from every online player, outside claimed territory and on walkable ground, else the unit waits), and within the mover's seed reach of that player (100 m, hunting-grounds A4) aggro is seeded and Hunt and the game's aggro take over; with no eligible player they stay where they stand · twins: hordes D5

D4 Spread on each kill

a victim, player or NPC not ours, whose killer resolves (the unit itself, else its EntityOwner, as the scoreboard does) to a living roamer or horde unit of the running apocalypse spawns `perKill` new roamers within 3 m of the victim's position, which hunt like the others; a spread roamer joins the hotspot nearest its spawn point: the hotspot whose perHotspot top-up counts it (D3), whose roamers it hunts with, and the hotspot it is recorded and rebuilt at (D11, D12); a spread roamer's own kills spread too; no spread unit is queued while live roamers are at `roamers.max`, while `perMinute` spread units were queued in the last 60 s, or while MaxTrackedUnits is full; 1000 qualifying kills inside one minute queue at most `perMinute` units · twins: hordes D5

D5 Hordes rise and grow

D6 Hordes march to the goal

each risen, living horde has a front that starts at its hotspot and advances along the straight line to the goal at `marchSpeed` m/s; the front holds while any player stands within 60 m of it (the horde fights, through Hunt) and advances otherwise, except that no hold applies while the front is within goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, so a player standing at the goal cannot keep a horde out and block a loss; whenever no player has been within 100 m of the horde for 10 s, at least 80 % of its living units stand within 30 m of the front (the mechanism is the builder's, set by the D24 spike, see S-9); a front whose next point has no walkable ground within 30 m holds and logs "horde <k> held: no walkable ground ahead" once per hold; the march's progress is the share of the origin-to-goal distance covered · twins: roamers D3

D8 Won, lost or survived

D12 Resume after a restart

(design §9 D37) at boot the boot marker sweep removes every marked survivor as before; then, when the record names a definition that still exists, is startable, is still an Apocalypse with at least as many hordes as had risen, and whose EndsUtc is in the future (the run's clock keeps running while the server is down, so each horde missed while down rises once at the resume, in order, spaced by the existing wave staging; catch-up rises top up roamers once, after the rebuild, not once per rise), the run resumes with its StartedUtc, EndsUtc, resolved goal (from the record, never re-read from the definition or a player), hotspots, hordes risen, fronts and scoreboard; once the boot sweep's despawns have drained, its units are rebuilt fresh through SpawnTracker: each hotspot's living roamers and each living horde's living count at its front, each `bosses` entry's living count as that entry's prefab with its own modifiers, and the rest as regular units; if the drain is not finished 5 min after the sweep, the resume proceeds anyway after one warning "[nyar] apocalypse <id>: boot sweep not drained after 5 min (<n> left); resuming", the queued despawns keep draining under MaxDespawnsPerTick and the rebuild stays inside MaxTrackedUnits and MaxSpawnsPerTick (proceed rather than refuse: the leftover units are already marked, queued and counted against the caps, so no safety rule is weakened, while a refusal would end a days-long run for a slow drain); it logs "event <id> resumed after restart (horde <r>/<c>, <u> units to rebuild)"; any other record logs "apocalypse <id> not resumed: <definition gone|disabled|changed|ended while down>" and is removed, and an EndsUtc already past ends the run as survived in the log, with no unit spawned
