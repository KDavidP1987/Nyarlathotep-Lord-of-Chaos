## Components
- C1 · **An outbreak that starts where the faction lives** an admin defines or picks an apocalypse, starts it, and it rises from hotspots found among the faction's own natives, which it never touches · D1 D2 D18 D25
- C2 · **Roamers that spread** hunting units that rise at each hotspot, go after the nearest players anywhere on the map, multiply on their kills and can never run away past the caps · D3 D4 D25
- C3 · **Hordes that march** growing hordes, announced at their origin, that walk to the goal and fight the players they meet · D5 D6 D7 D24 D26 D36
- C4 · **A result and a scoreboard** the run ends won, lost or survived, by an admin stop or by the kill switch, and shows who fought best · D8 D9 D10 D26 D27
