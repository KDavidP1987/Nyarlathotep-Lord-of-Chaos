# DoD: Automation — interval, player-action triggers and fan-out

**Size:** L. The plan touches several modules:
- **Logic:** Model, Validation, Schedule (the interval clock), Engine (TriggerRouter), Spawning (PlayerPick, WaveGate), DataStore (state.json), CommandArgs, Authoring, EventAdmin, ApiLines, Wire, Hooks, and a new Logic/PlayerTriggers.cs.
- **Services:** TriggerBus, EventScheduler, EventRuntime, WaveAction, HealthMonitor, SpawnTracker (a read), and Patches/DeathEventPatch.cs.
- **Resources:** templates.json.
- **Docs and tools:** the Raphael contract (api 6), design §6, tools/rollback-drill.ps1 and the preflight registries.

It adds three trigger types and one optional action key to events.json and one optional key to state.json (both stay SchemaVersion 1). It changes the wire (api 5 → 6). It adds no dependency and no data file. It ships one release, 0.8.0, starting from 0.7.0 (event-spawns).

**Planned:** interactively. This plan is a child of the approved Epic `nyarlathotep` (Epic A34). Its `## Child constraints` › automation entry governs it, with Epic Business rules 1, 3, 6, 8 and 9 and Epic D16 and D24. The owner settled the scope in plan mode on 2026-09-29 (S-1 to S-3). The assumptions marked reversible below are decisions taken inside that scope without asking, each with its fallback.

**Request:** the owner's decisions of 2026-09-29 (plan mode, all recommendations taken), after asking that "95% of the events should be automatic. Either based on a trigger or a schedule", working "at scale":
- 1A: a new child `automation` (0.8.0), built next, before stats.
- 2A: an Interval trigger `{ "type": "Interval", "minMinutes", "maxMinutes" }` (a random next start after each end, kept across restarts in state.json); per-player fan-out of AroundPlayer waves `fanOut: { maxInstances, minSpacing }`, always within the caps; the triggers RegionEntered (per-player cooldown) and FactionKills (N kills of a faction within W seconds).
- 3A: the tick budget stays promised at the default caps (150 tracked); 500 tracked is proven at Epic close (Epic D24).

## Purpose & typical use
The admin of a V Rising server with Nyarlathotep installed wants the world to act on its own. Today an event starts from a fixed clock time, the day or night edge, a V Blood kill, or an admin command. The admin wants to say "every hour or so, something hunts the players", "when someone walks into the Cursed Forest, it answers", and "if a player slaughters twenty bandits, the bandits strike back".

The job: most events run without an admin. This is the owner's 1.0 goal of about 95 % automatic events, from a schedule or from what players do, working on a busy server.

It extends what exists. The trigger bus (Services/TriggerBus.cs) gains three trigger types beside Manual, Schedule, GameTime and VBloodKilled. The AroundPlayer location of event-spawns (0.7.0) gains fan-out, so one event can reach several players spread over the map. Everything else (conditions, caps, scope, the kill switch) applies unchanged.

## Use cases
### Typical
- **Roaming hunters:** an Interval event, 60-90 min, AroundPlayer with fanOut 3 and Hunt. About every 75 minutes, up to three players at least 150 m apart each get a hunting squad nearby. With 30 players online, three spread-out groups are reached per start instead of one.
- **Border watch:** a RegionEntered event on Cursed Forest with playerCooldownMinutes 30. A player crossing into the forest gets an ambush wave around them; the same player crossing back and forth does not re-trigger it within 30 minutes; another player entering while it runs finds it already active and triggers nothing (one instance).
- **Bandit reprisal:** a FactionKills event on Faction_Bandits, 20 kills in 300 s. A player clearing a bandit camp fast gets a Hunt wave around them.
- Each start logs "event <id> started by <trigger>", announces as configured, and ends, despawns and redraws on its own.

### Minimal stretch
- An Interval definition with minMinutes equal to maxMinutes runs at a fixed period (D1, D2).
- A RegionEntered trigger with only `type` and `scope` uses the default cooldown of 30 minutes (D8).
- A fanOut on a server with one eligible player spawns one group, as without fanOut (D5, D6).
- A FactionKills trigger with kills 3 and windowSeconds 10 is the smallest allowed (D10).
- The admin who enables one Interval template and never touches it again gets one start per interval until the definition is disabled; nothing accumulates, since state.json holds one next start per waiting Interval definition (D2, D31).

### Maximal stretch
- **Volume (9.1):**
  - 100 Interval definitions: the clock is one comparison per definition per tick; MaxConcurrentEvents (default 3) bounds what runs, and a refused start redraws its next start, so there is no retry storm (D2, D14).
  - 30 players and 20 RegionEntered definitions: one player read and one region lookup per player every 5 s, then a set lookup per definition (D9). RegionIndex.RegionOf is a box test per polygon (regions D15).
  - A 100-player fight: every death passes the kill rule only when a FactionKills definition is startable, and each counter is bounded (D11).
  - fanOut 10 with 20 units per group: the whole wave is clamped to MaxUnitsPerWave (default 20, ceiling 50) and MaxTrackedUnits (default 150), and SpawnTracker spawns at MaxSpawnsPerTick (default 10), so fan-out spreads a wave over more players, never more units (D6, D7).
  - The tick budget at the default caps is measured with fan-out, the scan and the kill feed running (D21).
- **Abuse (9.2):**
  - A player walking in and out of a region to farm waves: the per-player cooldown stops it, and a relog does not reset it (D9).
  - A player farming FactionKills with their own event's units: our units never count (D11).
  - A player killing with a familiar or summon: counted as the owner's kill, as a direct kill would be (D11).
  - An admin with fanOut 10 and MaxUnitsPerWave 50: at most 50 units per wave, under MaxTrackedUnits and its hard ceiling of 500 (Epic Business rules 9, D6).
  - Many players entering at once: one instance runs; the rest are dropped with no line (D14).
  - A player waiting just outside a region for the cooldown to lapse, then stepping in: one wave per playerCooldownMinutes at most; with playerCooldownMinutes 0 only the one-instance rule and conditions.cooldownMinutes bound it, which the README states (D8, D14).
  - A clan taking turns with several accounts, each with its own cooldown: bounded by one instance at a time and the definition's conditions.cooldownMinutes (D14); a per-clan cooldown belongs to anti-farming.
  - With `shared: true` the last killer is the focus, so a player may take the last kill to draw the wave onto themselves or their group; accepted, since the wave is the event's purpose (D13).
- **Repeated or parallel use (9.3):** one player's two triggers within 5 s start once (Epic Business rules 8, D14); a due Interval next fires once (D2); `event set` on the new fields is idempotent through the editor's reload (D16).

## Business rules
1. **Interval (D1, D2):** the next start is drawn uniformly in whole seconds between minMinutes and maxMinutes after the moment the event was last seen inactive: its end, a refused start, the first poll after an enable or a reload, or the boot. It is kept in state.json and survives a restart when still in the future; one at or before the boot is redrawn, so downtime is never replayed (Epic Business rules 6). While an instance runs there is no next start. A purge's cooldown refuses a due start, which redraws (D22). A server clock stepped forward makes every passed next due at once: each fires once, bounded by MaxConcurrentEvents, and each refused one redraws (D2, D14).
2. **Fan-out (D4, D5, D6):**
   - Only with an AroundPlayer location.
   - Each wave picks up to maxInstances eligible players (event-spawns D16's rule), each at least minSpacing metres from every one already picked, the focus player first.
   - Each group is rolled on its own. The whole wave, all groups together, is clamped to MaxUnitsPerWave and the free MaxTrackedUnits, as any wave (S-4); the allowed units are dealt round robin to the groups in pick order.
   - A claimed centre skips only its group.
   - Each group carries its own HuntTag and hunts by Hunt's rule (the nearest eligible player within Hunt range of its units), so a group engages its own centre player first unless another stands nearer.
   - It is one instance with one wave count: the one-instance rule is unchanged.
3. **RegionEntered (D8, D9):** an entry is a move from a region outside the trigger's scope to one inside it, seen between two 5 s scans; a transit through a region shorter than one scan (bat form across a corner) is not an entry, by design; an unmapped gap counts as outside every scope; a first sighting is not an entry; the per-player cooldown counts from the last entry that reached a start attempt and, keyed on the platform id, outlives a relog.
4. **FactionKills (D10, D11):** a kill counts when the killer is a player (or a player's familiar, summon or projectile), the victim is of a listed faction, and the victim is neither our unit, a minion nor the killer; with a scope, the victim stands in it. N kills within W seconds fire, per player or, with shared, for all players together; the counter then empties.
5. **Focus (D13):** a player-action start remembers the triggering player for its AroundPlayer waves, in memory only; if that player is not eligible, any eligible player is picked.
6. **One start path (D14):** every new trigger goes through TriggerRouter.Candidates and EventRuntime.StartEvent, so Epic Business rules 1's precedence (kill switch > General.Enabled > pillar switch > caps > the definition) and conditions apply as for every trigger.
7. **Numbers stated twice:** the ranges live in Logic/Validation.cs and are quoted here and in the READMEs; Validation.cs is authoritative (Epic Business rules 10). The default caps are in Logic/Limits.cs.
8. **Precedence between the new rules (4.4):**
   - A trigger reaching an active event is dropped before the conditions (D14).
   - The conditions come before the caps; the caps (MaxUnitsPerWave, MaxTrackedUnits) bound the whole fanned-out wave, and maxInstances and minSpacing only choose the groups inside that bound (S-4).
   - The per-player cooldown is checked before the dedupe.
   - The owner decides exceptions by editing the definition or the cfg caps; no rule has an exception in code.
9. **"Every X" sets (4.5):**
   - "Every Interval definition" is TriggerRouter.Candidates(set, Interval); IntervalClock.Load prunes state.json against it at boot, and each poll prunes an id that has left it (D2).
   - "Every end path" needs no hook: the clock sees the event inactive on the next poll, whatever ended it (D2, D22).
   - "Every player" of a scan is PlayerQuery.Read's rows: connected users with a readable character. A player the read leaves out makes no entry and loses its region row (D9, D15).
   - "Every kill" is a DeathEvent row of DeathEventListenerSystem, the same source as VBloodKilled (DEV_REMINDERS #26). A unit destroyed without dying (despawn, purge) raises none and counts nothing, which is intended (D11).
   - "Every group" of a wave goes through one clamp (D6).
   - "Every new line" is listed in D19 and D27.
   - "Every path" this child writes is in Rollout › Paths walked (D25).

### States
- **Empty and first run (7.1):**
  - With no Interval, RegionEntered or FactionKills definition, the clock, the scan and the kill rule do nothing: the scan phase reads no player (D9) and the kill rule returns at once (D11).
  - A new Interval definition waits a full draw before its first start, never at once (D2).
  - The first scan after boot sees every player for the first time, so it makes no entry (D9).
- **Loading:** nothing polls or scans before Core.IsReady (foundation ready guard). IntervalClock.Load runs once, after the definitions load (D2).
- **Partial:** a fanned-out wave spawns over several ticks within MaxSpawnsPerTick. A group whose centre is claimed is skipped alone (D6).
- **Error states:** the D15 streaks and health entries.
- **Concurrent use (7.2):**
  - Two players enter one region in the same scan: the first in scan order starts the event and the second finds it active and is dropped (D14).
  - A kill and a region entry for one event in one tick: whichever runs first starts; the other is dropped.
  - An admin's `event start` and an Interval due in the same tick run in tick order on the main thread; the second is "already active".
  - I (Claude) am an actor only through the build: sessions change the dev server's state.json while it runs, so every session is wrapped by the snapshot (D25).
- **Stale data, cancel, re-entry (7.3):**
  - A reload that changes minMinutes or maxMinutes redraws a next beyond the new maximum (D2); a next still inside the new range is kept, even when the minimum was raised or lowered, since it was drawn fairly and the next draw uses the new range.
  - A reload that removes a definition drops its next, rows and counters on the next poll (D2, D11).
  - Stop, purge, fault cancel, natural end, restart and uninstall end an instance with its focus and units (D22).
  - The purge cooldown pushes the next start back (D22).

### Permissions
- **Actors (2.1):**
  - **Players** reach the feature only by playing: walking into a region or killing NPCs. They cannot start, stop or configure events and see only announcements, which never name or locate them (D14, D19).
  - **Admins** (VCF's admin list) author the definitions through events.json or `.nyar event set`, read `event info`, and hold the kill switch (D16, D20).
  - **The System actor**, the trigger bus, starts events through Gateway.Run with `Actor.System` (D14, D20).
  - **Raphael clients** read the new trigger values through the existing admin-only api reads (D17).
  - **The operator** installs and removes the DLL.
- **Unauthorized path (2.2):** a non-admin's `.nyar event set` is refused by VCF's admin check with no change, as today (event-library; -AuthSuite, D20).
- **Ownership (2.3):**
  - A trigger started by one player's action is not owned by that player: the instance's waves may reach other players by fan-out, and ending it is the admin's or the clock's (D13, D22).
  - When the focus player leaves, the focus passes to any eligible player (D13).
  - Definitions belong to the admins together, as today.

### UX
- **Where and discovery (11.1):**
  - The three templates (`.nyar template list`, D18), `.nyar event set` fields (D16), `.nyar event info` (D16), both READMEs' trigger tables (D24), and `.nyar help` for the event group.
- **Feedback (11.2):**
  - A set reply names the new value; a refusal names the rule (D16).
  - `event info` shows the next Interval start (D16).
  - The log names every start's trigger (D3, D12). Refusals are throttled (D14).
- **Accessibility (11.3):** every line is under 480 bytes and holds no '<' or '>'; chat is keyboard-driven, and the game offers no screen reader, which the README states (D27).
- **Activation (11.4):**
  - Nobody asks for these triggers: they activate from the clock (Interval), a player's move (RegionEntered) or kills (FactionKills), only for enabled definitions of an enabled pillar.
  - What shows they did: "event <id> started by Interval|RegionEntered|FactionKills" in the log, the announcements, and `event info` (D3, D12).
  - Should-not-activate cases:
    - a login inside the region, and a re-entry inside the cooldown (D9);
    - kills of our own units, or of another faction (D11);
    - a disabled definition, or an event already running (D14).

## Security
- **Authorization on every path (10.1):**
  - The trigger bus is an indirect path: it starts events as System through Gateway.Run, which applies the pillar switch, General.Enabled and the purge cooldown (D14, D20).
  - Chat writes are admin-only (event-library). The static checks of D20 fail on a mutating call outside the gateway.
  - No allowed program's options change here.

## Performance
- **Budget (13.1):** the scheduler tick averages under 5 ms at the default caps (Epic D24, S-3), measured with fan-out, the scan and the kill feed (D21). The hot paths:
  - the 5 s scan: one player query, one RegionOf per player, one set lookup per definition; HuntAction's own 5 s tick runs a second player query, and D21 measures both, so the duplicate read is accepted rather than shared;
  - the kill rule per death, run only while a FactionKills definition is startable;
  - the fan-out pick, once per wave, over at most the online players.
  The spawn itself stays SpawnTracker's, within MaxSpawnsPerTick.
  D21 times the scheduler tick only. The kill rule runs in DeathEventListenerSystem's postfix, outside that tick, and is bounded by construction: it returns at once unless a FactionKills definition is startable, then costs one killer and owner read, one faction read and a set lookup per FactionKills definition, with bounded counters (D11). The slow-tick warning does not see it; the game's own frame time does.
- **Limits (13.2):**

| Limit | Value | Where it came from | Valid case it excludes |
|---|---|---|---|
| fanOut.maxInstances | 2-10 | MaxConcurrentEvents' ceiling (10) as a scale | an 11th group on a 100-player server |
| fanOut.minSpacing | 50-500 m | twice Hunt's 60 m range at the low end; half a region at the high end | two groups in one camp |
| Interval minutes | 5-1440 | the shortest session window; a day | an event every 2 minutes |
| kills | 3-500 | a group of three; a long grind | 2 kills |
| windowSeconds | 10-3600 | one fight; one hour | a day-long count |
| playerCooldownMinutes | 0-1440 | none to a day | a week-long cooldown |
| kill counters | 200 per definition, 2000 in all | 2× a full 100-player server; ten such definitions | a 201st player counted at once, or an 11th busy definition (the stalest is dropped) |
| refusal lines | 1 per definition per 60 s | the slow-tick warning's rate | a second reason within the minute (counted) |

At a bound: a validation bound disables the definition with its reason (D1, D4, D8, D10); a memory bound drops the stalest row (D11); a cap clamps with its cap line (D6).
