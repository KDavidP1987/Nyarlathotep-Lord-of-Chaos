# DoD: Wave sets — per-wave units and stats, waves that wait to be cleared, the event scoreboard

**Size:** L. A schema change (an optional `action.waveList` and `action.scoreboard` in events.json) across several modules:
- **Logic:** Model, Validation, Engine (the wave schedule, the victory end), Spawning (the roll and the deal), SpawnLedger (a wave number per unit), AnnouncerCore, EventAdmin, CommandArgs, Authoring, Messages, and a new Logic/Scoreboard.cs.

- **Resources:** templates.json. **Tools:** tools/rollback-drill.ps1, tools/preflight-checks.json.
No new external dependency.
**Planned:** interactively. The owner answered the four questions on 2026-10-04 (design §9 D38-D41, all option A); the Halloween track's process applies (design §9 D34: one review round, two at most; Codex once on the child's diff; vrclient scenarios as the in-game evidence).
**Request:** "custom spawn templates with per-wave units and stats and a scoreboard; level controllable separately from the stats" (the owner's players, Epic A35); Epic Child constraints › Halloween track: "**wave-sets** (0.9.0) — an optional `waveList` on SpawnWaves where each wave has its own units, each unit its own level/levelDelta and stat multipliers, and each wave starts after `afterSeconds` or when the previous wave is cleared; plus the per-event scoreboard (kills of the event's units, deaths while the event runs, participation) announced at the end, scoreboard only, no item grants (D35); every unit still through SpawnTracker under the caps."

## Purpose & typical use
A server admin wants a fight with a shape: three waves of undead, the second waiting until the players have finished the first, the last one with a big, tough leader; and at the end, a line in chat saying who did best. Today a spawn event repeats the same units every `intervalSeconds`, with one set of stats for every unit, and nobody sees a result. "Set up a graveyard fight for Saturday with three waves and a scoreboard" is the sentence.

The job: per-wave unit lists with stats per unit (level separate from the multipliers, as the players asked), waves that wait to be cleared, an early end when every wave is beaten, and a scoreboard of kills and deaths. It extends SpawnWaves (event-spawns, automation) and coexists with the units form, which stays exactly as it is. The scoreboard is the first piece of the Halloween events (swarm, revenge and apocalypse reuse it) and is absorbed by the stats child later (Epic A35).

## Use cases
### Typical
The admin uses the `graveyard-waves` template, sets its location with `.nyar event set graveyard-waves location here`, enables it and starts it. Wave 1 spawns around a player. The players kill it, and wave 2 comes on the next tick. Wave 3's leader goes down, the event ends with "all waves defeated", and chat shows the top 3 with their kills and deaths.

### Minimal stretch
- A waveList with one wave and one unit: the event spawns it, ends when it dies (D7), and shows the scoreboard when `scoreboard` is true.
- A waveList without `scoreboard`: no kill is read and nothing is shown (D2).
- No modifiers anywhere: every unit at its prefab's base level and stats (D3).
- Used once and never again: the template stays disabled, the rows die with the event, and nothing is written to disk (D8, D12).

### Maximal stretch
- Volume: 10 waves × 10 entries × 50 copies are clamped per wave by MaxUnitsPerWave (default 20) and by the free MaxTrackedUnits slots, as today (D3); 200 participants at most per event (D8); the scan cost is one ledger query per active waveList event per tick (D5, D23).
- Abuse: a player can farm kills by tagging a familiar onto the fight, which counts for them by design (D8, S-5); an admin can't top the board unless the server opts in (D9). A wave that never clears (a unit stuck in a wall) is bounded by afterSeconds when set and always by the event's duration (D4).
- Repeated use: one instance per definition at a time, unchanged from 0.8.0 (Epic Business rules 8); a second `event start` gets 0.8.0's reply. A waveList re-set through chat while the event runs takes effect at the next start (the running instance keeps its definition, as every other key does).

## Business rules
1. **Forms.** A SpawnWaves action is either the units form (units, waves, intervalSeconds, modifiers) or the waveList form; never both (D1). Location, radius, scope, behaviour, loot, allowTerritory, fanOut, unitLifetimeSeconds and scoreboard apply to every wave of either form.
2. **Start rule** (D40, D4): wave 1 at the start; wave k ≥ 2 at the earlier of (wave k−1's decision + afterSeconds) and (wave k−1 cleared) when whenCleared is true; at wave k−1 cleared when neither key is set; at wave k−1's decision + afterSeconds when only afterSeconds is set; nothing after the event's end. A decision time is the tick the wave was spawned, skipped or zero-rolled (event-spawns D29). Whether the earlier time is due is checked once per tick; with both keys a clear at 30 s starts the wave at 30 s, a clear at 200 s with afterSeconds 120 starts it at 120 s.
3. **Cleared** (D5): decided, and no pending order and no tracked unit of that event and wave remain. A skipped, zero-rolled or zero-queued wave is cleared at once (D4, D5); a blocked wave (pillar off, territory map down: NoWave) is not decided and its successor waits for it.
4. **Victory** (D41, D7): the last wave cleared, every wave queued at least one unit (fought, not skipped), the event still running → ended at the next tick as a natural end with "all waves defeated". A skipped, zero-rolled or zero-queued wave still releases its successor (rule 3) but rules out the early end: that event runs to its duration and its natural end shows the scoreboard.
5. **Counting** (D39, D8): kills of the event's own units by players or their familiars and summons; deaths of players to the event's units; participants have one or more of either; rank by kills desc, deaths asc, first credit earlier. A unit killing itself, a kill of a native unit and a death to a native unit count nothing.
6. **Admins** (D38, D9): dropped from the rows and the totals unless Scoreboard.IncludeAdmins is true, decided at each credit by User.IsAdmin.
7. **Showing** (D41, D11): natural end, all waves defeated, admin stop; never purge, restart, pillar off or fault cancel. The scoreboard does not depend on EventBanners: the definition's `scoreboard: true` is the opt-in.
8. **Precedence** (4.4): the caps (MaxUnitsPerWave, MaxTrackedUnits, MaxConcurrentEvents) beat the wave list; a clamped wave is still one wave and is cleared when its queued units are gone. The kill switch beats everything: a purge ends the event with no scoreboard. Validation beats chat: a field the validator would refuse is refused by `event set` with the validator's reason (D14). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
9. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every schedule reader" is the files that read a SpawnWavesAction's Waves or IntervalSeconds (recon list: Logic/Engine.cs, Logic/AnnouncerCore.cs, Logic/ApiLines.cs, Logic/EventAdmin.cs, Logic/Messages.cs, Services/EventRuntime.cs, Services/WaveAction.cs), checked by D6's source scan, which reads every *.cs under Nyarlathotep/Nyarlathotep (tracked or not, obj/ excluded); "every removal path" of a unit is the six of D5 (SpawnTracker.Died, the despawn queue, LifeTime, "removed by the game", spawn failure, event end); "every end path" is the seven of D11; "every name output" is the outputs PrivacyTests enumerates (Messages, WaveLines, AdminLines, PushLines, ApiLines, EventLines, state.json).

Nothing new is persisted: state.json is unchanged (D12), and every running wave set ends on boot as before (Epic S-11; only the apocalypse child narrows it, design §9 D37). Migration (3.4): none; every new key is optional and absent means 0.8.0's behaviour.

### States
- **7.1** Empty: a waveList event with no participant shows "no player scored" (D10); first run: a waveList event's first wave spawns at the start (D4); partial: a wave clamped to fewer units is cleared when those are gone (D5); error: a refused definition is disabled with its reason (D1), a failing read holds its health entry (D13, D18).
- **7.2** Concurrent use: two admins editing one definition race through the event library's single writer, as today (event-library); two events with scoreboards keep separate rows (D8); a kill whose victim belongs to event A and killer to event B credits A only (D8). The planner (Claude) and the build tooling act only on the dev server between sessions, wrapped by the snapshot (D26).
- **7.3** Cancel and re-entry: stop shows the scoreboard, purge and restart do not (D11, D22); a restart cancels the instance and the boot sweep removes its units (D22); a re-set of waveList while running applies at the next start; a definition edited to remove `scoreboard` mid-run keeps counting for the running instance (the instance holds its definition).

### Permissions
- **2.1** Admins: start, stop, purge, edit (`event set`), all existing admin-only commands (D14 adds fields to an admin command). Players: no new command; they are counted on the scoreboard. The server operator: the cfg key (D9). Unauthenticated users: none.
- **2.2** A player running `.nyar event set` gets the existing admin-only refusal (Epic D3), nothing is logged as an admin action.
- **2.3** Ownership: a definition belongs to the server; any admin edits it. A running instance keeps its definition even when another admin edits it (7.3).

### UX
- **11.1** Discovery: `.nyar event templates` lists graveyard-waves (D16); the README's Wave sets section (D25); `event info` shows each wave (D15).
- **11.2** Feedback: every `event set` refusal names the field (D14); the scoreboard lines (D10); the victory log line (D7); rendered in real chat in Session 1 (D21).
- **11.3** Accessibility: plain text, at most 480 bytes a line (D10, D14, D15); no colour carries meaning.
- **11.4** Activation: the scoreboard appears only for a definition with `scoreboard: true` at the three showing paths (D11); a wave set starts by any of the existing triggers (Manual, Schedule, Interval, …), its waves by the start rule (D4). It must not activate for a units-form event, for an event without the key, or on a purge (D11, D2).

## Security
- **10.1** Authorization: the new chat fields go through `.nyar event set`, which is admin-only (Epic D3, existing AuthSuite); no new command, no new wire command (D17); the cfg is the operator's file.
- **10.2** Injection: names go to chat only through TextSink.Name (angle brackets and control characters removed, 20 characters) (D10); prefab names are checked against the catalog (D1); field paths are parsed, never evaluated (D14).
- **10.3** Secrets: no credential is added; the release keeps the token sentinel and the privacy grep (D27).
- **10.4** Personal data: character names appear only in the scoreboard chat lines (D12); platform ids live in memory only; no log line, push or file carries a name or id (D12); admins are left out by default (D9).

## Failure & observability
- **12.1** What the admin sees: a refused definition's reason in `.nyar event list` and the log (D1); "scoreboard: kill read failing" in `.nyar status` (D13); a held wave's schedule line (D18). What they do next: fix the key, or stop the event; the kill switch always works.
- **12.2** Logged: each wave line with its number (existing), "event <id> ended: all waves defeated (<s> of <n> waves)" (D7), "event <id> scoreboard: <p> players, <K> kills, <D> deaths" (D11), the once-per-streak failures (D13, D18). "event <id> wave <k> cleared" when a wave clears (D5's read, one line per wave, always logged; D20 orders by it).
- **12.3** Knowing it is broken: the health line's entries (D13), -LogCheck after every session (D26), the vrclient scenario (D20-D22).
- **12.4** Failing cases: every test and cmd item names its fails-when; each has a ControlCases row (D19); the planted-fault runs of each new test are recorded in the audit's post-audit lines; the dry runs before approval are in the Log. Empty inputs: an empty waveList (refused "action.waveList needs 1-10 waves"), an event without the key (D2), an empty scoreboard ("no player scored", D10), a -TimingSpan log with no windows (fails, D23), a -DependencySuite with no tests (fails, D18).

## Performance
- **13.1** Budget: the existing tick budget, 5 ms average at the default caps (automation D21); the hot paths are the death postfix (one ledger lookup per death per scoreboard event, only while one runs) and WaveSchedule (one WaveCleared per running waveList event per tick, a count over the ledger's units of that event: at most MaxTrackedUnits). D23 measures it.
- **13.2** Bounds: waveList 1-10 waves, 1-10 entries a wave, count 1-50 (D1); MaxUnitsPerWave per wave and MaxTrackedUnits overall (D3); 200 scoreboard players per event (D8: a 200-player event is beyond any server this mod targets; the cap came from TriggerLimits.CountersPerDefinition; the excluded case is a 201st participant, who still counts in the totals); afterSeconds 10-3600 (a wave waiting more than an hour is out of an event's typical 10-60 minutes).
