4. Business rules & invariants

What the plan says under “Business rules”

  1. **Units are ours (CLAUDE.md › Spawn & buff safety):** every change in this plan lands on units SpawnTracker spawned and will despawn: - modifiers ride on the unit's marker buff (MultiplyBaseAdd, A7 of foundation); - the level is written on the unit at spawn, and Hunt's AggroBuffer seeds are HuntAction's own entries on our units (D13). No native NPC and no prefab is changed. Modifiers scale the prefab baseline through MultiplyBaseAdd, so they never compound (Epic Business rules 4).
  2. **Ranges (D6):** - chance 0.05-1.0; - level 1-120, or levelDelta -5..5 (Epic Business rules 4, never both); - multipliers 0.5-3.0 with two decimals (S-8); - Hunt range 10-60 m (S-9); - AroundPlayer minDist 10-60 and maxDist 15-80, minDist < maxDist; with behaviour Hunt, maxDist ≤ the Hunt range, so the picked player is always within it ("action.location.maxDist must be at most behaviour.range for Hunt").
  3. **Spawn points (D17):** - walkable-spawns' search (0.5.1) keeps a walkable ring point; this plan adds that, unless allowTerritory, it must also be unclaimed, otherwise the search moves it. - A centre in claimed territory skips the wave (unless allowTerritory). - A failed territory map skips the wave (fail closed: a player's property is never at risk from a lookup failure); a failed walk check stays fail open (walkable-spawns D5).
  4. **Loot and XP (Epic S-10, Business rules 6):** loot is off unless the event sets `loot: true`; XP stays vanilla.
  5. **Behaviours:** at most one per event (D6). This child has one, **Hunt:** seeded every 5 s within range of the wave centre, at most 5 targets (D13). Guard and Ambush come with child spawn-extras (S-15). Behaviours never outlive the wave: seeding stops at every end path (D23).
  6. **AroundPlayer (D16):** one random eligible player per wave; eligible means online, alive, outside claimed territory and not in PvP combat. The player is never named or located in any output. No eligible player means no wave.
  7. **Durations are hard (Epic Business rules 3):** nothing in this plan extends a unit's life. Due time, LifeTime with Age, DestroyWhenDisabled and the boot sweep are unchanged (D23).
  8. **Precedence (4.4, D29):** - The caps and controls of Precedence.StartBlocker and WavePlan.Split win over everything here. - A skip for claimed territory or no eligible player comes before the roll. - The chance roll comes before the caps (D8). - allowTerritory lifts only the territory rule, never a cap. - The admin who writes the definition decides every exception. There is no per-player exception.
  9. **"Every" sets (4.5):** - "Every spawned unit" is every order SpawnTracker.Spawn receives; its only callers are RequestWave (event waves) and SpawnManual (`.nyar spawn`). - "Every end path" is natural end, stop, fault cancel, purge, restart and uninstall (D23). - "Every new key" is the OnlyKeys list of ParseSpawnWaves and ParseLocation, which D6's test enumerates. - "Every new control" is D22's ControlCases list. - "Every path this child writes" (D27) is computed by `-Paths -DeclaredOf event-spawns`: git diff --name-only against the commit named in the audit's Step 1 pre-audit heading, plus the untracked, ignored and server paths holding a file written after that commit, the %TEMP%\nyar-* folders present, every %TEMP%\nyar-<name> folder a tools/ script or the child's records name, and every tools/ line taking the %TEMP% root (the temp-root scan); known exclusion: a repository, server or AppData path created and deleted inside one step (S-13, design §9 D18); owner: the builder, the step's /code-review and Codex cross-inspection read the diff for such writes. - "No token or SteamID" (D31) is computed over the Secrets check's set (the tracked and untracked tree, dist/, build/ and the build log) and the git grep over tracked files before each push; "no player position" (D16) over the message builders and log calls PrivacyTests enumerate (every Services/ and Logic/ call site that formats a wave, hunt or pick line).

D13 Hunt seeks nearby players

while a Hunt wave has live units, Services/HuntAction.cs every 5 s adds one AggroBuffer entry per unit for each target of Logic `HuntPlan.Targets(players, centre, range)`: online, alive player characters within range of the wave centre, nearest first, at most 5; no entry is added twice for the same unit and player, and HuntAction never seeds a player the unit's AggroBuffer already holds (that entry stays the game's); it removes a seed only while exactly its own entry for that player remains; on each 5 s tick HuntAction re-reads the eligible targets and removes the AggroBuffer entries it seeded (its own seed set per unit) for a player no longer a target (dead, disconnected, out of range, or ineligible by D16's territory and PvP-combat rule: `HuntPlan.Targets` takes the players with their in-territory and PvP-combat flags, the territory flag read on each tick from the TerritoryMap built for the wave, which changes only when a castle is placed or removed), never an entry the game added; with no eligible player it removes all its seeds and adds none (Logic `HuntPlan.Diff(seeded, targets)` returns the adds and removes); after a partial write (an exception or a unit or target gone mid-write) HuntAction's seed record, keyed by unit entity and target, stays authoritative: on the next tick it drops only the record entries missing from the unit's AggroBuffer, never adopts an entry from the buffer (the buffer cannot tell a seed from a game entry for the same player), and skips a unit or target that no longer Exists; an entry for a recorded player whose fields differ from what HuntAction wrote (the game replaced the seed between two ticks) is the game's: its record is dropped and the entry left; seeding stops at the wave's end, stop, purge or restart
