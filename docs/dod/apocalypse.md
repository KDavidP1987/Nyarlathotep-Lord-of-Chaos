# DoD: Apocalypse — hotspots, spreading roamers, marching hordes, win or lose, the scoreboard, resume after a restart

**Size:** L. A schema change (a third action kind, `"type": "Apocalypse"`, in events.json, and an optional `Apocalypse` record in state.json) across several modules:
- **Logic:** Model, Validation, Engine (the third action kind, the outcomes), Limits, Messages, AnnouncerCore, EventAdmin, CommandArgs, Authoring, Scoreboard (persisted rows), DataStore (the state record), and new Logic/Apocalypse.cs (hotspots, spread, horde schedule, march, outcomes, resume rule, rolling lifetime).
- **Services:** a new Services/ApocalypseAction.cs, SpawnTracker, EventRuntime, EventScheduler, HuntAction, Announcer, HealthMonitor, Persistence, Patches/DeathEventPatch.cs, Core.cs (the resume after the boot sweep).
- **Resources:** templates.json. **Tools:** tools/rollback-drill.ps1, tools/preflight-checks.json, tools/vrclient/NyarDevTools (the spike's dev command, never shipped).
No new external dependency.
**Planned:** by a drafting session for the orchestrating session on 2026-10-11, from the Epic (A35), design §9 D33-D37 and the recon below. The owner settled the outcomes, the spread, the resume data and the downtime clock on 2026-10-11 (design §9 D44, D45). The Halloween track's process applies (design §9 D34: one review round, two at most; Codex once on the child's diff before release; vrclient scenarios as the in-game evidence).
**Request:** Epic Child constraints › Halloween track: "**apocalypse** — an outbreak MVP: hotspots where the faction's native NPCs stand, roaming hunting units that spawn more on each kill under a cap, growing hordes announced at their origin that march to a goal point, win when every horde wave is defeated and lose when a horde reaches the goal, the scoreboard, durations of hours or days, and resume after a restart (D37, narrowing S-11 for this event type alone); no native NPC is converted." Design §9 D37: "The zombie apocalypse resumes after a restart: its run, wave number and scoreboard persist in state.json and its units are rebuilt fresh after the boot sweep; every other event still ends on boot."

## Definition of Done
- [ ] D1 · **Apocalypse schema** a definition may carry `"action": { "type": "Apocalypse", … }` with `faction` (a Faction_* name the catalog knows), `units` 1-10 entries of `prefab`, `count` 1-50 (its weight in a pick), `chance` 0.05-1.0 and an optional `modifiers` object under the ParseModifiers rules, `hotspots { count 1-5, minNatives 3-50, minSpacing 100-3000 }`, `roamers { perHotspot 1-10, perKill 0-2, max 1-100, perMinute 1-30, huntRange 20-60 }`, `hordes { count 1-10, firstAfterMinutes 0-1440, everyMinutes 5-1440, size 5-50, growth 0-20 }`, `goal { location (Point with x, z and optional y, or Admin), radius 10-100, name 1-32 printable characters }`, `marchSpeed` 0.5-5.0 m/s, an optional `scope` and `scoreboard` (absent = true); for this kind `durationSeconds` is 600-604800 (7 days) while every other kind keeps 30-7200; an Admin goal needs a Manual trigger; a definition whose last horde would rise at or after the duration (firstAfterMinutes × 60 + (count − 1) × everyMinutes × 60 ≥ durationSeconds) is refused with "action.hordes: last horde rises at or after the duration"; an unknown key names its path ("unknown field action.<path>"); every refusal disables the definition with one reason that names the field and fits 120 bytes · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests EventValidationTests Apocalypse (fails when: a bound is off by one, a 604801 s apocalypse or an 7201 s wave event loads, an Admin goal loads with a Schedule trigger, a definition whose last horde rises at or after its duration loads, an unknown key is accepted or named without its path, or a units entry skips a ParseModifiers rule)
- [ ] D2 · **Hotspots found, natives untouched** at the start, Logic/Apocalypse.cs Hotspots.Pick groups the faction's eligible native NPCs (Logic/Empowerment.cs Eligibility rules: not a V Blood, not a prisoner or non-combatant, not a unit of ours) standing inside the action's scope into 100 m cells, keeps cells with at least minNatives, ranks them by count (ties by x, then z), takes up to `count` at least minSpacing apart, and puts each hotspot at the mean position of its cell's natives; fewer than one hotspot refuses the start with "no hotspot: fewer than <n> <faction> natives in one place in scope" and logs it; the read never writes, buffs, moves or destroys a native entity, checked by a source scan of the hotspot reader that fails on any EntityExtensions write or destroy call and reports "native scan: 0 files" over an empty set · twins: none — hotspots are found once per run, for every unit kind alike · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests Hotspots (fails when: a cell under minNatives is picked, two hotspots stand closer than minSpacing, a native outside the scope or a unit of ours counts, an empty world starts the run, or the scan misses a planted write in the reader)
- [ ] D3 · **Roamers at the hotspots** at the start and at each horde rise, every hotspot is topped up to `perHotspot` living roamers drawn from `units` by weight and chance, spawned within 15 m of the hotspot on walkable ground through SpawnTracker, each hunting players within `huntRange` of its hotspot (event-spawns' Hunt); live roamers never exceed `roamers.max`; a spread roamer (D4) counts towards its nearest hotspot's perHotspot at a top-up · twins: hordes D5 · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests Roamers (fails when: a hotspot gets more than perHotspot at a top-up, a spread roamer is left out of its nearest hotspot's count, the top-up skips a hotspot, the total passes roamers.max, or a roamer is queued outside SpawnTracker's ledger)
- [ ] D4 · **Spread on each kill** a victim, player or NPC not ours, whose killer resolves (the unit itself, else its EntityOwner, as the scoreboard does) to a living roamer or horde unit of the running apocalypse spawns `perKill` new roamers within 3 m of the victim's position, which hunt like the others; a spread roamer joins the hotspot nearest its spawn point, which is its Hunt centre, the hotspot whose perHotspot top-up counts it (D3) and the hotspot it is recorded and rebuilt at (D11, D12); a spread roamer's own kills spread too; no spread unit is queued while live roamers are at `roamers.max`, while `perMinute` spread units were queued in the last 60 s, or while MaxTrackedUnits is full; 1000 qualifying kills inside one minute queue at most `perMinute` units · twins: hordes D5 · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests Spread (fails when: a kill by a native or by another event's unit spreads, a roamer or horde unit kill does not, a spread roamer joins a hotspot other than its nearest, the 60 s window lets more than perMinute through, roamers pass roamers.max, or a chain of spread kills escapes either cap)
- [ ] D5 · **Hordes rise and grow** horde k (1..`hordes.count`) rises at start + firstAfterMinutes + (k−1) × everyMinutes at hotspot ((k−1) mod hotspots) + 1, with size + (k−1) × growth units clamped by MaxUnitsPerWave and the free MaxTrackedUnits slots; a horde that queues no unit (cap full) has not risen and is retried every 60 s, so a full cap can never fake a defeated horde; a rise due after the run's end never comes · twins: roamers D3 · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests Hordes (fails when: a horde rises at another time or hotspot, its size ignores growth or a clamp, a zero-queued horde counts as risen or defeated, or a rise past the end spawns)
- [ ] D6 · **Hordes march to the goal** each risen, living horde has a front that starts at its hotspot and advances along the straight line to the goal at `marchSpeed` m/s; the front holds while any player stands within 60 m of it (the horde fights, through Hunt) and advances otherwise, except that no hold applies while the front is within goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, so a player standing at the goal cannot keep a horde out and block a loss; whenever no player has been within 100 m of the horde for 10 s, at least 80 % of its living units stand within 30 m of the front (the mechanism is the builder's, set by the D24 spike, see S-9); a front whose next point has no walkable ground within 30 m holds and logs "horde <k> held: no walkable ground ahead" once per hold; the march's progress is the share of the origin-to-goal distance covered · twins: none — roamers stay at their hotspots by design · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests March (fails when: the front moves while a player is within 60 m outside goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, holds for a player inside goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, stops with none near, overshoots the goal, moves at another speed, or the held line repeats within one hold)
- [ ] D7 · **Announced at the origin** a horde's rise queues (AnnounceQueue, Info, whatever EventBanners says, as the scoreboard does) "<event name>: a horde of <n> rises in <region> and marches on <goal name>" (the region from RegionMap, "the wilds" when the hotspot lies in none), each 25 % of a march "<event name>: the horde is <p> % of the way to <goal name>", and its defeat "<event name>: horde <k> of <c> destroyed"; no line names or locates a player; names go through TextSink; each line fits 480 bytes with a 64-byte event name and the longest region name · twins: none — roamers are not announced (they are the background of the outbreak) · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests Lines (fails when: a line exceeds 480 bytes, names a player, keeps "<", misses its region or goal name, or a 25 % step is announced twice)
- [ ] D8 · **Won, lost or survived** the run ends at the next tick as **lost** when a horde's front is within goal.radius and at least one of its living units stands within goal.radius + 30 m of the goal; as **won** when every horde of `hordes.count` has risen and has no living unit and no pending order; as **survived** when its duration ends first; lost beats won on the same tick; each outcome ends through Engine.Complete or Expire with the grace cleanup of every roamer and horde unit and one `event-end` push, logs "event <id> ended: <won|lost|survived> (<r> of <c> hordes risen)", queues its outcome line ("the living have won", "<goal name> has fallen", "the living held out") and then the scoreboard · twins: roamers D3 · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests Outcomes (fails when: a run ends won while a horde is unrisen or alive, ends lost with no unit at the goal, a full-cap retry fakes a win, won and lost on one tick read won, an outcome sends no event-end push or a second one, or a unit is left out of the cleanup)
- [ ] D9 · **Every end path** an admin `.nyar event stop` ends the run with the scoreboard and no outcome line; `.nyar purge confirm`, `.nyar pillar spawns off` and a fault cancel end it with neither; each of those four removes the resume record, so a later restart resumes nothing, and a purge or pillar off issued during a resume's drain wait (after the boot, before the rebuild, D12) does the same and rebuilds nothing; `.nyar event disable` of a running apocalypse does not end the live run (an admin uses `event stop` for that), but the disabled definition is not resumed after a restart (D12); a restart keeps the record (D12); every other event kind is still cancelled by a restart (Epic S-11) · test: Nyarlathotep.Tests ApocalypseTests EndPaths (fails when: one of the four paths leaves the resume record, a purge or pillar off during the resume's drain wait leaves a record or lets the rebuild spawn, `event disable` ends a live run or its definition resumes after a restart, a silent path queues a scoreboard, stop queues none, or a restart cancels an apocalypse or resumes a wave event)

## Purpose & typical use
A server admin wants the Halloween night the owner's players asked for: the undead rise where the undead already live, small packs hunt anyone nearby and grow every time they kill, and every half hour a bigger horde forms and marches on a place the admin chose. The players have to break each horde before it gets there. "Run the zombie apocalypse on Saturday night, marching on the starter town" is the sentence.

The job: hotspots found at run time from the faction's own NPCs (no native is converted), roamers that spread on kills under a cap, hordes that grow and march to a goal, a clear result (won, lost or survived), the wave-sets scoreboard, and a run that lasts hours or days and survives a server restart. It extends event-spawns (Hunt, walkable spawn points), regions (scope, region names), wave-sets (the scoreboard, the cleared read) and automation (triggers). It is the MVP of the outbreak child (docs/features/OUTBREAK.md), which keeps conversion, the Dracul horde, boss copies and castle assaults for after Halloween (design §9 D33).

## Use cases
### Typical
The admin runs `.nyar template use zombie-apocalypse`, stands in the town square and runs `.nyar event enable zombie-apocalypse` and `.nyar event start zombie-apocalypse`, so the goal is the admin's position. The mod finds three undead hotspots, puts four roamers at each, and announces nothing yet. A player wandering near Farbane's graveyard is hunted; when the roamers kill them, another roamer stands up where they fell. Ten minutes in, chat says "Zombie apocalypse: a horde of 8 rises in Farbane Woods and marches on the living". The horde walks towards the town; players meet it on the road and fight it, which holds it in place. Four hordes later, the last one dies, and chat shows "the living have won" and the scoreboard. The server restarted once in the middle: the run came back on its own, the horde reappeared where its front had been, and the scores kept their kills.

### Minimal stretch
- One hotspot, one horde, perKill 0: no spread, one march, won or lost (D3, D4, D5, D8).
- An apocalypse with `scoreboard: false`: nothing is counted, no scoreboard is shown, the outcome line still is (D10, D8).
- No hotspot in scope: the start is refused with its reason, nothing spawns (D2).
- Used once and never again: the template stays disabled; the record is removed at the end; units have a rolling 2 h lifetime and a boot sweep, so nothing lingers (D9, D11, D13, D18).

### Maximal stretch
- Volume: 10 hordes of up to 50 plus 100 roamers are clamped by MaxUnitsPerWave and MaxTrackedUnits (default 150, ceiling 500) and spawned at MaxSpawnsPerTick (D5, D14); the README says to raise the caps for bigger hordes, and D29 measures the tick at 50 tracked; 500 tracked stays Epic D24's.
- Abuse: players farm spread roamers for scores; the spread is bounded by roamers.max and perMinute (D4), and the scoreboard counts each kill once (D10). A player AFK at the goal cannot block a loss: no hold applies while a horde's front is within goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, so the horde reaches the goal and the run ends lost (D6, D8). An admin cannot start a second apocalypse (D14).
- Repeated use: one apocalypse at a time, one instance per definition (D14); a restart resumes the one record (D12); a purge clears it so nothing resumes (D9).

## Business rules
1. **One action kind.** `"type": "Apocalypse"` is a third action kind beside SpawnWaves and Empower; its definition runs under the Spawns pillar switch (S-6) and its own validation (D1). A definition carries exactly one action, as today.
2. **Hotspots** (D2): found once per run at the start, from the faction's eligible natives in scope (cells of 100 m, minNatives, minSpacing, ranked by count), kept for the run and its resumes; read-only towards the natives.
3. **Roamers** (D3): topped up to perHotspot at each hotspot at the start and at each horde rise, never above roamers.max; they hunt within huntRange of their hotspot.
4. **Spread** (D4, design §9 D44): each qualifying kill, of a player or an NPC not ours, by a roamer or a horde unit queues perKill roamers at the victim's position, each joining the nearest hotspot, under roamers.max, perMinute in any 60 s and MaxTrackedUnits.
5. **Hordes** (D5): horde k rises at start + firstAfterMinutes + (k−1) × everyMinutes at the next hotspot in turn with size + (k−1) × growth units under the caps; a horde that queued nothing has not risen and retries every 60 s.
6. **March** (D6): the front moves at marchSpeed m/s along the straight line from its hotspot to the goal, holding while a player is within 60 m of it, except within goal.radius + 60 m of the goal, where nothing holds it; its units keep up with it (S-9).
7. **Outcomes** (D8, design §9 D44): lost when a horde's front is within goal.radius with a living unit within goal.radius + 30 m of the goal; won when every horde has risen and none has a living unit or pending order; survived when the duration ends first. Lost beats won on one tick. An admin stop shows the scoreboard without an outcome; purge, pillar off and a fault cancel show nothing (D9).
8. **Resume** (D12, design §9 D37, D45): only an apocalypse resumes, from its record, after the boot sweep, with units rebuilt fresh; its clock keeps running while the server is down and each horde missed while down rises once at the resume, in order; the resume waits for the boot sweep's drain up to 5 min, then proceeds with one warning; its resolved goal comes from the record; every other event is cancelled on boot (Epic S-11).
9. **Lifetime** (D13): every apocalypse unit has a rolling LifeTime of at most 2 h plus the drain margin, renewed while the run lives.
10. **Precedence** (4.4): the kill switch beats everything (a purge ends the run, removes its record and despawns staged); the caps beat the definition (a clamped horde is still that horde; a zero-queued one has not risen); a definition edited while running keeps the running instance on its old definition, and a resume reads the current one, refusing it when it changed kind or has fewer hordes than had risen (D12); validation beats chat (D16). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
11. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every unit of the run" is the ledger's orders and units carrying its event id, from every spawn path (start top-up, rise top-up, spread, horde rise, horde retry, resume rebuild) and every removal path (died, despawned, LifeTime, removed by the game, failed spawn, end cleanup, purge), reconciled by D15's tally; "every end path" is won, lost, survived, stop, purge, pillar off, fault and restart (D8, D9, D12); "every name output" is the outputs PrivacyTests enumerates plus the record (D19); "every native write" is checked by D2's source scan over the hotspot reader.

## Performance
- **13.1** Budget: the existing tick budget, 5 ms average (automation D21, design §9 D29). The hot paths: the march (per living horde each tick: one front step, one player-distance check against PlayerQuery's rows, and a hop of at most the horde's units every 5 s), the spread (one ledger lookup per death while an apocalypse runs), the record write (≤ 1 per second, ≥ 30 s apart in a march), and the rolling renewal (at most one LifeTime write per unit per 30 min). ## Rollout
### Shipping
One release, 0.12.0 (steps 1-5), a GitHub pre-release; the owner publishes to Thunderstore. Revenge's release (0.11.0) is its starting point (S-11, S-12).

Everything still ships off: no existing definition uses the Apocalypse type, the template is disabled, the Spawns pillar is off by default (Epic D4). Who turns it off:
- any admin, with `.nyar event stop`, `.nyar pillar spawns off` or `.nyar purge confirm`, each of which ends the run and removes its record so nothing resumes (D9); `.nyar event disable` does not end a live run (use `event stop`), but a disabled definition is not resumed after a restart (D12);
- the operator, by removing the DLL: units expire within about 2 h by their rolling LifeTime (D13) and the record is ignored.

### Compatibility
- events.json and state.json stay SchemaVersion 1; the action kind and the record are optional (D1, D11).
- The wire keeps its api value (D20); a Raphael client sees an apocalypse as an event with `wave=<risen>/<hordes>`.
- Every other event still ends on boot (D9, Epic S-11).

### Rollback
- **In the repository:** `git revert --no-edit v0.11.0..v0.12.0` for the whole child, drilled by the rollback gate with -Plan (D32); commits in the range that are not this child's are re-applied with `git cherry-pick`, listed from `git log v0.11.0..v0.12.0` minus the commits touching this child's exclusive paths.
- **On the dev server during the build:** every session is wrapped by tools/dev-snapshot.ps1 (D34).
- **On a server:** purge first (`.nyar purge confirm`, which removes the record), then install the 0.11.0 DLL. Without the purge, 0.11.0 ignores the record, cancels the instance on boot as it does every event, sweeps the units by their marker, and drops the record on its next state write. An Apocalypse definition is disabled with the action-type reason, every other definition runs; this is the drill's accepted difference (D32). Nothing is written that 0.11.0 cannot read past, so this stays possible after any amount of use.
- **Published release:** tags and releases are never deleted. A bad release is withdrawn by retitling it, and versions move forward only (Epic S-19).
- **Commit range:** v0.11.0..v0.12.0
