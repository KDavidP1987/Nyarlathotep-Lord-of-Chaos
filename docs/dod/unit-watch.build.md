# Build view: Unit watch — stuck units recovered unseen, then removed, so a wave never hangs

Generated from `unit-watch.md` by `dod-wbs.mjs --build`; it is not the source of truth. Change the plan, then run `--build` again.

**North Star:** a server admin's wave events and apocalypse never hang on a unit no player can reach: such a unit is moved back unseen and, failing twice, removed with no kill credit so the next wave comes, inside the tick budget and the caps, with an admin command for it now, released with apocalypse as 0.13.0 before 2026-10-31; finished when D1-D33 have evidence.

Risk: (none stated)

## Components
- C1 · **Stuck detection** a unit of ours that stands unreachable near the players, or sits where there is no ground, is found, and a unit that fights or stands by design is not · D2 D3 D4 D19 D23 D24 D34
- C2 · **Hidden recovery** a stuck unit is put back on walkable ground near its group, a player or its anchor, never in view, beside a player, in a castle or out of its scope · D5 D6 D23 D26
- C3 · **Removal without credit** a unit that stays stuck after two tries leaves, its wave moves on, and nobody gets a kill, a trigger or a spread for it · D6 D7 D8 D24 D26
- C4 · **Wave stall backstop** a wave that waits for its clear and goes quiet for N minutes clears its leftovers the same way · D9 D25
- C5 · **Admin control** the cfg keys, `unstick` in chat and on the wire, and the watch counts in `event info` · D1 D12 D13 D14 D15 D25
- C6 · **Safe to ship** switches and caps win, nothing left behind, one line per change, names nowhere, guarded reads, the tick budget, older events unchanged, a drilled rollback and a joint release · D10 D11 D16 D17 D18 D20 D21 D22 D27 D28 D30 D31 D32 D33
- C7 · **Fair in play** the players never see a unit pop in or out beside them and no wave hangs, as the owner judges · D29
