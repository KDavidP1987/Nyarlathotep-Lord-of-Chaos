# DoD: Swarm — a contained arena of growing spider waves with a kill-to-death winner

**Size:** L. A schema change (three optional keys, `action.arena`, `action.winner` and `action.growth`, in events.json) across several modules:
- **Logic:** Model, Validation, Scoreboard, Messages, CommandArgs, DefinitionEditor, Authoring, EventAdmin, a new Logic/Arena.cs (the containment rule) and a new Logic/Growth.cs (the wave generator, about two days of the plan).
- **Services:** EventScheduler (one tick phase), SpawnTracker (positions of an event's units and the return), HealthMonitor (one entry), EventRuntime (the winner at the showing end paths).
- **Resources:** templates.json. **Tools:** tools/rollback-drill.ps1, tools/preflight-checks.json, tools/vrclient/scenarios/swarm*.vrs.
No new external dependency, no new cfg key, no new command.
**Planned:** interactively, as a draft for the owner's decisions (S-12 settled 2026-10-11, design §9 D42 option B: the growth key). The Halloween track's process applies (design §9 D34): one review round, two at most; Codex once on the child's diff before release; a vrclient scenario as the in-game evidence; the owner judges only look and feel (D18).
**Request:** Epic Child constraints › Halloween track: "**swarm** — a contained area event (Point plus radius) whose waves grow, with kills and deaths per player and the best kill-to-death ratio named as the winner." Epic A35, the players testing with the owner: "spider spawning within a region". Design §9 D33: "a contained spider swarm with a K/D winner".

**North Star:** a server admin gets a spider swarm that stays inside its arena, grows wave by wave from one base wave and names the player with the best kill-to-death ratio, built on 0.9.0's wave sets and scoreboard with no parallel machinery, released as 0.10.0 well before 2026-10-31 (aim 2026-10-18); finished when D1-D25 have evidence.

## Purpose & typical use
A server admin wants a Halloween fight the players can find: "put a spider swarm in the woods by the old mill for Saturday night, and tell us who did best". The players asked for spiders spawning in one area; the owner set the shape (design §9 D33): a contained swarm whose waves grow, with a kill-to-death winner.

Today an admin can already build growing waves with 0.9.0's waveList, wave by wave by hand, and get a kills scoreboard, but the units chase players across the map and the scoreboard rewards whoever kills most, not whoever fights best. The job: keep the swarm in its arena (D3), let one base wave grow by a few units and levels per wave without writing every wave (D24, D25; the owner's choice, design §9 D42), ship a spider-swarm template that uses it (D11), and name the best kill-to-death ratio (D5, D6). It extends SpawnWaves and the wave-sets scoreboard and coexists with every 0.9.0 event, which runs unchanged without the three new keys.

## Use cases
### Typical
The admin stands in a clearing and runs `.nyar template use spider-swarm`, `.nyar event enable spider-swarm` and `.nyar event start spider-swarm`. Six spiders at level 30 appear around them; players fight inside the 40 m arena. Each wave comes when the last is cleared (or after 120 s), three spiders bigger and three levels higher each time, up to 18 at level 42 in wave 5 (D25). A player who runs out of the arena is not followed past its edge (D3). When the last spider of wave 5 dies the event ends "all waves defeated" and chat shows the top 3 by kills and "Spider swarm winner: <name>, K/D 7.50 (15 kills, 2 deaths)" (D6).

### Minimal stretch
- An arena on a units-form event with one wave of one unit: it spawns, is kept inside, and the event ends at its duration (D1, D3).
- `scoreboard: true` with no `winner`: 0.9.0's lines exactly (D6).
- `growth` with waves 2 and addLevels 1 only: two waves of the same units, the second one level higher (D24, D25).
- `winner` set, nobody kills anything: "no player scored" only (D6).
- Used once: the template stays disabled, the rows die with the event, nothing is written to disk (D8, D11).

### Maximal stretch
- Volume: a growth event's largest wave is at most 50 units by validation (D24) and is clamped to the configured MaxUnitsPerWave and the free MaxTrackedUnits slots like any wave (D25); an arena event at the default caps tracks at most 150 units; the arena check reads each live unit's position once per 5 s (D3, D19). A fourth arena event is refused by MaxConcurrentEvents (default 3), as today.
- Abuse: a player can stand outside the arena and shoot in; their kills count wherever they stand (D39, S-9) and the units do not follow them out (D3). A player can farm a perfect ratio with one kill and no death; the ratio of a player who kills more and dies once beats it as soon as it is higher (D5, S-6). A unit stuck outside (a wall, a cliff) is returned on every check; the event's duration bounds it (D3, D4).
- Repeated use: one instance per definition, as 0.8.0 (Epic Business rules 8); an event stopped and restarted inside its grace never moves or credits the old instance's units (D3, wave-sets A3).

## Business rules
1. **Arena** (D1, D3): a circle of arena.radius (15-60 m) around the centre: the Point location's x and z, or the starting admin's position for an Admin location. AroundPlayer has no fixed centre and takes no arena. The spawn radius and a Hunt range fit inside it, so units spawn inside and hunt only players inside (HuntPlan.Targets reads the range from the centre).
2. **Kept inside** (D3, S-5): every 5 s (HuntPlan.IntervalSeconds, the existing behaviour cadence) a unit of the running instance farther than arena.radius + 10 m from the centre is returned to within action.radius of it. The 10 m slack (S-13) lets a unit finish a swing at the edge without being moved.
3. **Winner** (D5, S-6): over every row, players with at least one kill, highest kills ÷ max(1, deaths), then more kills, fewer deaths, earlier first credit. Admins and the 201st player follow wave-sets D8 and D9.
4. **Showing** (D7): the winner line rides with the scoreboard: wave-sets D11's three showing paths, none of its four silent ones.
5. **Growth** (D24, D25, S-12, design §9 D42): a units-form action with `growth` has `waves` waves; wave n is the base wave with every entry's count raised by (n−1)·addUnits and its level by (n−1)·addLevels (an absolute level at most 120); waves 2-n start intervalSeconds after the previous one, or at its clear as well with whenCleared. The generated list is an ordinary waveList to everything after the parser. Growth lives on the SpawnWaves action, beside `units`, because it reuses the units form's own keys (units and modifiers as the base, waves, intervalSeconds) and so needs no new count or timing key; it is not on `arena` because growth is useful without containment.
6. **Precedence** (4.4): the caps (MaxUnitsPerWave, MaxTrackedUnits, MaxConcurrentEvents) beat the wave list, a generated wave and the arena; the kill switch beats everything (a purge ends the event with no scoreboard and no winner, D7, D17); validation beats chat (D9); the arena never moves a unit it did not spawn for this instance (D3). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
7. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every unit of the running instance" is SpawnTracker's ledger filtered by event id and instance start (the boundary wave-sets A3 drew), so `.nyar spawn` units and an earlier instance's units are outside it (D3); "every end path" is wave-sets D11's seven (D7, D17); "every name output" is the outputs PrivacyTests enumerates (D8); "every reader of the three keys" is Validation, Logic/Growth.cs, CommandArgs, DefinitionEditor, Authoring and EventAdmin's info lines, the arena phase and ScoreboardLines, each with an item (D1, D2, D24, D25, D9, D10, D3, D6); "every generated wave" is waves 1 to `waves` of D25's list; "every arena event" is one in the units form, the waveList form or the growth form, each with its own D3 fails-when.
- whole: none — the winner is one row picked from the scoreboard; no list of parts here adds up to a total (wave-sets D8 owns the totals)
- siblings: Point location, Admin location

Nothing new is persisted: state.json is unchanged (D8) and every running swarm ends on boot (Epic S-11). Migration (3.4): none; the three keys are optional and absent means 0.9.0's behaviour (D1, D2, D24).

### States
- **7.1** Empty: a winner event nobody scored in shows "no player scored" only (D6); first run: the first wave spawns inside the arena (D15); partial: a clamped wave is still kept inside (D3); error: a refused definition is disabled with its reason (D1, D2), a failing check holds its health entry (D4).
- **7.2** Concurrent use: two arena events keep their own units (D3 moves only its instance's); two admins editing one definition race through the event library's single writer, as today; an event restarted inside its grace never moves the old instance's units (D3). Claude and the build tooling act on the dev server only between sessions, wrapped by the snapshot (D22).
- **7.3** Cancel and re-entry: stop shows the scoreboard and winner, purge and restart show neither (D7, D17); no arena check runs for an ended event (D17); a restart cancels the instance and the boot sweep removes its units (D17); an edit of `arena`, `winner` or `growth` while the event runs applies at the next start (the running instance keeps the waves generated at its start, wave-sets D13).

### Permissions
- **2.1** Admins: start, stop, purge and `event set` the arena, winner and growth fields (D9, admin-only). Players: no new command; they are kept out of nothing and counted on the scoreboard. The server operator: no new cfg key. Unauthenticated users: none.
- **2.2** A player running `.nyar event set` gets the existing admin-only refusal (Epic D3, AuthSuite in D9); nothing is logged as an admin action.
- **2.3** Ownership: a definition belongs to the server; any admin edits it; a running instance keeps its definition when another admin edits it (7.3, D9). An Admin-location arena belongs to the start, not to the admin who started it: it stays where it began if that admin leaves (D3).

### UX
- **11.1** Discovery: `.nyar event templates` lists spider-swarm (D11); `event info` shows the arena, the winner, the growth rule and every generated wave (D10); the README's Swarm section (D21).
- **11.2** Feedback: refusals name the field (D1, D9); the winner line in the product's plain chat voice (D6), rendered in real chat in Session 1 (D16).
- **11.3** Accessibility: plain text within 480 bytes (D6, D10); no colour carries meaning; the ratio uses a dot in every locale (D6).
- **11.4** Activation: the arena acts only for a definition with `arena` while it runs (D3), the winner only for `winner: KillDeath` at a showing end (D7); a swarm starts by any existing trigger. It must not act for an event without the keys, after an event ends, or on a purge (D1, D7, D17).
- **11.5** Design bar: the spiders look like a swarm held in an arena and the winner line reads well; a qualitative bar judged by the owner on the dev server (D18).

## Security
- **10.1** Authorization: the arena, winner and growth fields go through `.nyar event set`, admin-only (Epic D3, AuthSuite, D9); no new command, wire command or cfg key (D12).
- **10.2** Injection: the winner's name reaches chat only through TextSink.Name (D6); the field values are parsed as integers, booleans or one keyword, never evaluated (D1, D2, D9, D24); growth cannot outgrow the caps: its last wave is at most 50 units by validation and every wave is clamped at spawn (D24, D25); prefabs in the template are checked against the catalog (D11).
- **10.3** Secrets: none added; the release keeps the token sentinel and the privacy grep (D23).
- **10.4** Personal data: the winner's character name appears only in the winner chat line (D8); no log, push or file carries a name, platform id or player position; the arena log line carries counts and a distance only (D3); admins stay out by default (wave-sets D9).

## Failure & observability
- **12.1** What the admin sees: a refused definition's reason in `.nyar event list` and the log (D1, D2, D24); a generated wave's size and level in `event info` before the start (D10); "arena: check failing" in `.nyar status` (D4). What they do next: fix the key, or stop the event; the kill switch always works.
- **12.2** Logged: "[nyar] arena <id>: returned <n>, farthest <d> m" when a check moved units (D3); "[nyar] arena <id>: check failed: <message>" once per streak (D4); the scoreboard log line with ", winner K/D <r>" or ", no winner" (D7); wave-sets' wave and cleared lines unchanged.
- **12.3** Knowing it is broken: the health line's entry (D4), -LogCheck after every session (D22), the vrclient scenario (D15-D17).
- **12.4** Failing cases: every test and cmd item names its fails-when and has a ControlCases row (D14); each new test's planted fault is run in its step and recorded in the audit; the dry runs are in the Log. Empty inputs: an arena object without radius (refused, D1), an empty growth object (refused "must add units or levels", D24), a winner event with no participant ("no player scored", D6), an arena event with no live unit (the check does nothing and logs nothing, D3), a -TimingSpan log with no windows (fails, D19), a -DependencySuite with no category (fails, D13). Thresholds cite their source: the 10 m slack (S-13), 5 s (HuntPlan.IntervalSeconds), 15-60 m (S-13), 5 ms (automation D21), 50 units (Limits.MaxUnitsPerWave's ceiling), level 120 (LevelArg's clamp), addLevels at most 5 (MaxLevelDelta).

## Performance
- **13.1** Budget: the existing tick budget, 5 ms average at the default caps (automation D21). Recon measurement: wave-sets Session 1's timing span (docs/dod/wave-sets.md Log, 2026-10-05) held nine windows under 5 ms with 20 tracked, Hunt on, and one window at 8.524 ms from a 176.8 ms hunt-phase tick (wave-sets A7, its cause still under diagnosis). The arena's hot path is one position read per live unit of an arena event every 5 s, at most MaxTrackedUnits; D19 measures it.
- **13.2** Bounds: arena.radius 15-60 (S-13: the game drops a target beyond about 86 m, RESEARCH_NOTES › AggroConsumer, and Hunt's range is 10-60; the excluded case is an arena wider than 60 m, which Hunt could not cover); growth's addUnits 0-10 and addLevels 0-5 per wave and its last wave at most 50 units (D24; the excluded case is a growth that could never spawn in full under any MaxUnitsPerWave); the template's generated waves at most 20 units each (D11) so the default MaxUnitsPerWave never clamps it; the generator runs once per load, at most 10 waves of 10 entries (D25); the scoreboard's 200 rows (wave-sets D8).

## Out of scope
- **Considered and excluded (15.1):**
  - Containment to a world region's outline (a polygon) instead of a circle: Epic Child constraints say "Point plus radius"; a region swarm is a Point inside the region with `action.scope` naming it (regions D5, the wave centre must lie in the scope), which 0.9.0 already does (S-8).
  - An arena that moves with a player (AroundPlayer): no fixed centre to contain to (D1).
  - Keeping players in, or out, of the arena: only our own units are moved (CLAUDE.md › Spawn & buff safety).
  - Growth by the template's hand-written waveList alone, with no schema key (the draft's recommendation): the owner chose the growth key (design §9 D42, option B); a hand-written waveList still works for an admin who wants it.
  - Endless growth that repeats until the duration ends: growth makes `waves` waves (2-10), as a waveList does (D24).
  - A boss leading only the last generated wave (the draft template's Broodmother): every generated wave comes from one base wave (D25); an admin who wants one writes a waveList.
  - Growth of stat multipliers (maxHealth, power) per wave: levels and counts only, per design §9 D42.
  - Item rewards for the winner: design §9 D35, scoreboard only.
  - A push or wire field for the arena or the winner: the wire stays api 6 (D12).
  - Spider Banelings in the template: a self-detonation credits nobody (wave-sets D8), which would skew the ratio (D11).
- **Deferred (15.2):**
  - The player opt-out `.nyar stats hide` and lifetime stats: child `stats` (Epic D16, D40).
  - Revenge and apocalypse: children `revenge` and `apocalypse` (Epic A35).
  - The 500-unit budget run: Epic D24 at the Epic's close.

## Also considered
- **Compliance:** the winner's name is public in chat like the scoreboard's; admins stay out by default (wave-sets D9); the opt-out comes with stats and the README says so (D21).
- **Localisation:** English only; the ratio is written with a dot in every server locale (D6).
- **Running cost:** tick time only (D19).
- **Operational ownership:** the server admin; the runbook is the README's kill switch and uninstall sections.
- **Documentation and changelog:** the six surfaces of 0.10.0 (D21), docs/features/SWARM.md with the code, the contract sentence (D12), design §6 (D9).
- **Analytics:** the winner is per event only; lifetime stats are the stats child's.
- **Decommissioning:** nothing is replaced.
- **Support tooling:** `.nyar event info` (arena and winner), `.nyar status` (the check entry), `.nyar debug here` (the units inside the arena).
