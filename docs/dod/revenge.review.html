4. Business rules & invariants

What the plan says under “Business rules”

  1. **Heat** (D3): per definition and player, the sum of the points of counted kills within the last windowSeconds; a counted kill gives 1 point, a V Blood kill vbloodHeat; a point leaves windowSeconds after its kill (the decay, S-9); nothing is added during the cooldown.
  2. **Warnings** (D4): warning k when the heat rises to or past warnAt[k]; once per climb, the highest only when one kill passes two; re-armed below the threshold.
  3. **Due and start** (D5, D13): heat at or past `heat` makes the player due; a due player starts when eligible and when neither the definition nor the player is busy; the start resets the heat and starts the cooldown; a conditions refusal resets the heat only. An admin start targets the admin and touches no heat.
  4. **Tiers** (D6; design §9 D43): the tiers are the waves of the revenge's own SpawnWaves action: a waveList grows them per wave and starts each when the previous is cleared or after its afterSeconds (design §9 D40); the instance ends at all waves defeated (D41) or its duration.
  5. **Where** (D6, D36, design §9 D46): every tier around the target only, and every squad's Hunt seeds the target only; a companion who attacks a squad is fought through the game's own aggro, never seeded; a tier waits while the target is not eligible; never a centre, ring point or hunt target in claimed territory.
  6. **Who hears what** (D4, D7, D8; design §9 D43, D47): the target alone gets the warnings, the start line and the end line; the server gets the alert, which names no player and no place (Epic D16); the target stays anonymous while the revenge runs; the end-of-event scoreboard follows wave-sets D38 and names its top 3, the recorded exception (design §9 D47).
  7. **Precedence** (4.4): the kill switch beats everything (a purge ends the instance, empties all heat, and sends no line, D8); the caps beat revenge (a clamped tier is still one tier); one instance per definition beats a second target (held "busy", D5, design §9 D43); claimed territory beats the ambush (held, D6); validation beats chat (D11). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
  8. **Memory only** (D9; S-11): heat, due players, warning states and cooldowns are lost on every boot; the running instance is cancelled at boot as every event (Epic S-11).
  9. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every death read" is the one DeathEvent postfix (Patches/DeathEventPatch.cs), whose FactionKills read the heat shares (D10); "every player-facing revenge line" is the four builders of D9 (RevengeWarning, RevengeStart, RevengeEnd, RevengeAlertPool), enumerated by PrivacyTests' reflection over Logic/Messages; "every end path" of a revenge instance is the six of D8; "every wave pick" is Services/WaveAction.cs Pick through Logic PlayerPick (the only AroundPlayer pick) and Hunt's target choice, Logic/Spawning.cs HuntPlan.Targets (who a squad comes for, D6, design §9 D46); "every reader of a trigger type's fields" is the files that switch on TriggerType (recon: Logic/Validation.cs, CommandArgs.cs, EventAdmin.cs, ApiLines.cs, Hooks.cs, Engine.cs TriggerRouter, PlayerTriggers.cs), and a new type without a branch falls to their default, which D1, D11, D12 and D15's tests catch.

D1 Revenge trigger schema

a definition may carry `trigger: { "type": "Revenge", "factions": [1-5 Faction_ names], "heat": 3-500, "windowSeconds": 60-3600, "warnAt": [0-3 integers, strictly ascending, each 1..heat−1], "vbloodHeat": 0-100 (absent = 10), "playerCooldownMinutes": 0-1440 (absent = 30), "scope": … }`; factions use FactionNames' rules and reasons (deny list, unknown faction); each bound refused with its own reason ("trigger.heat must be 3-500", "trigger.windowSeconds must be 60-3600", "trigger.warnAt must be up to 3 ascending values below trigger.heat", "trigger.vbloodHeat must be 0-100", "trigger.playerCooldownMinutes must be 0-1440"); `shared` or any other key is refused "unknown field trigger.<key>"; every refusal disables the definition with one reason that names the field and fits 120 bytes

test: Nyarlathotep.Tests EventValidationTests Revenge (fails when: a bound is off by one, a descending or repeated warnAt or one equal to heat loads, `shared` loads, an unknown key is accepted, or an absent vbloodHeat or playerCooldownMinutes reads other than 10 and 30)

D2 Revenge action rules

a Revenge definition loads only with a SpawnWaves action whose location is AroundPlayer and whose behaviour is Hunt, in either the units form or the waveList form; an Empower action is refused "a Revenge trigger needs a SpawnWaves action", another location "a Revenge trigger needs location AroundPlayer", no Hunt "a Revenge trigger needs behaviour Hunt", `fanOut` "a Revenge trigger takes no fanOut" and `allowTerritory: true` "a Revenge trigger never spawns in claimed territory"; a definition with another trigger type loads as in 0.10.0

D3 Heat

Logic/Revenge.cs keeps per startable Revenge definition and per player (the platform id, memory only) the kill points within the last windowSeconds: a death counts for the player KillRule resolves (the killer, else its EntityOwner, else the player that owner follows) when the victim is of a listed faction and is not a player, a minion, one of our units or the killer itself, and with a regional trigger scope stands in a named region; a V Blood kill (Logic DeathRule.IsVBloodKill) adds vbloodHeat points, any other counted kill 1; a point leaves the heat windowSeconds after its kill; while the player's cooldown for that definition runs (D5) nothing is added; rows are bounded by TriggerLimits.CountersPerDefinition per definition and TriggerLimits.CountersInAll in all, the least recently updated row dropped first; these are a separate bound with the same constants: the heat rows live in Logic/Revenge.cs and never share or evict FactionKills' KillWindows counters, which keep their own 200 and 2000; a definition that leaves the startable set loses its rows

D4 Private warnings

when a player's heat for a definition rises to or past warnAt[k], that player alone gets warning k of n, once per climb: the private chat line Logic/Messages.RevengeWarning(k, n, factions) with {faction} the short names of the trigger's factions ("Bandits", joined by ", "), sent within 5 s of the kill; one kill that passes two thresholds sends only the higher; a threshold re-arms when the heat falls below it; each line is plain text within Wire.MaxBytes (480), names no player and carries no position; each sent warning logs "[nyar] revenge <id>: warning <k> of <n>" with no player identity

test: Nyarlathotep.Tests RevengeTests Warnings (fails when: a warning goes to any other player or to all, repeats within one climb, does not re-arm after a fall, two lines go for one kill, a line exceeds 480 bytes or holds a name, a digit pair or "<", or the log line names or locates the player)

no evidence line yet

D5 When revenge starts

a player whose heat reaches `heat` is due; on each 5 s scan a due player's revenge starts as a System player-action start focused on that player (automation D13) only when the player is online, alive, at a usable position, outside claimed territory, not in PvP combat and, with a regional action scope, inside it; no instance of that definition runs; no other running Revenge instance is focused on that player; and the controls allow it (pillar on, MaxConcurrentEvents and MaxTrackedUnits not full, the kill switch's cooldown not running); otherwise the player stays due while the heat stays at or above `heat`, and one "[nyar] revenge <id>: held (<territory|busy|target busy|ineligible|controls>)" line is logged per change of reason; a refusal by the controls is `controls` and keeps the player due with the heat untouched (it is not a conditions refusal); a start resets the player's heat for that definition and starts the playerCooldownMinutes cooldown; a start the definition's conditions refuse also resets the heat, without a cooldown; due players start in the order they became due

D6 Ambush around the target only

each wave of a running Revenge instance is centred minDist..maxDist from the focus player and never around another player; when the focus is not eligible by PlayerPick's rule (offline, dead, unusable position, claimed territory, PvP combat, outside a regional scope) the wave is held, neither spawned nor skipped (WaveOutcome.NoWave), and its successor waits, until the focus is eligible again or the event ends; ring points in claimed territory are refused as for every wave without allowTerritory (event-spawns D17); Hunt never seeds a player in claimed territory (HuntPlan.Targets); a Revenge instance's Hunt seeds only its focus player, whoever else stands within range (design §9 D46): a companion who attacks a squad is fought through the game's own aggro but is never seeded; the Hunt tick joins the two by resolving the focus's platform id (ActiveEvent.Focus) to its HuntCandidate key (`Logic/Spawning.cs:860`, a `long` entity handle), and a new HuntCandidate field that join needs is recorded as an amendment before it is built (review 2 F1); waves and hunts of definitions with another trigger keep 0.10.0's pick and HuntPlan.Targets' up-to-5 nearest players
