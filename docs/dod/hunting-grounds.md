# DoD: Hunting grounds — region-bound spawns, a region lock and a region hunt by hidden jumps

**Size:** L. A schema change (a fourth location type, `"location": { "type": "Region" }`, an optional `action.regionLock` and a behaviour type `RegionHunt`, in events.json) across several modules:
- **Logic:** Model (LocationType.Region, SpawnWavesAction.RegionLock, BehaviourType.RegionHunt), Validation, Spawning (the Region spots, the hunt's eligibility inside the regions), Engine (WavePlan for a Region group), CommandArgs, DefinitionEditor, Authoring, EventAdmin, and a new Logic/HuntingGrounds.cs (the spot draw, the lock rule, the hunt target and the jump rule, each pure).
- **Services:** WaveAction (Region groups), SpawnTracker (the lock's return and the jump on our own units), HuntAction (seeds inside the regions under the lock), EventScheduler (one tick phase), HealthMonitor (two entries), EventRuntime (the end paths), WalkCheck (the spot's height level).
- **Resources:** templates.json. **Tools:** tools/rollback-drill.ps1, tools/preflight-checks.json, tools/vrclient/NyarDevTools (the spike's dev command `.devhop`, never shipped), tools/vrclient/scenarios/hunting-grounds*.vrs.
No new external dependency, no new cfg key, no new command.
**Planned:** by a drafting session for the orchestrating session on 2026-10-12, from the Epic (A37, Child constraints › hunting-grounds), design §9 D48, D49 and D50 (the owner's decisions of 2026-10-12, recorded below as validated assumptions) and the recon below. Gaps the code and the decisions do not settle are labelled `reversible` with their fallback, because the Halloween track runs unattended between owner sessions. The Halloween track's process applies (design §9 D34): one review round, two at most; Codex once on the child's diff before release; vrclient scenarios as the in-game evidence; the owner tests look and feel and anything needing a player (the hunt, the jumps, the chase and return).
**Request:** Epic Child constraints › hunting-grounds: "Region-bound placement for SpawnWaves events: a region lock that brings an event's own units back inside its scope regions, as swarm's arena does for its circle; a Region location that spreads a wave's spawn points over the scope regions on walkable ground; a region hunt that sends the units after the eligible players inside the scope regions, past the game's own aggro reach of about 60 m, by a mover that moves only units we spawned. With an AroundPlayer location and a scope, the lock gives the region ambush; with a Point location, the point spawn that spreads through the region. Every unit goes through SpawnTracker under the caps, and no line names or locates a player (D16). Apocalypse's march and its map-wide roamers (design §9 D49) reuse this mover." Design §9 D50 (owner, 2026-10-12): "A unit outside its regions is brought back to a walkable spot just inside the border near where it left and forgets players outside (as swarm's arena); the long-range hunt moves our units in hidden jumps only while no player is within about 100 m, and within about 60 m the game's own pathing and aggro take over; each group goes after the nearest eligible player in its regions (castle territory skipped); a region ambush picks only players standing inside the regions."

**North Star:** a server admin can bind a wave event to named map regions (spawn anywhere in them, keep the units in them, and have the units hunt the players inside them by jumps no player sees), using the existing SpawnWaves locations and caps, with nothing left behind at any end, released as 0.11.0 before revenge and before 2026-10-31; finished when D1-D32 have evidence.

## Purpose & typical use
A server admin wants the map's regions to feel dangerous on their own: "for Halloween night, let the spiders own Dunley Farmlands: spawn them all over it, keep them there, and have them hunt anyone who walks in". After swarm's Session 3 the owner listed five region-bound modes (design §9 D48): a region roam, swarm's point arena, a region ambush, a point spawn that spreads through its region, and apocalypse's map-wide spread.

Today a SpawnWaves event spawns at one Point, around the starting admin or around a player, can be limited to a region by `action.scope` (regions D6: its spawn points must lie inside), and swarm's arena keeps units in a circle. Nothing keeps units inside a region's outline, nothing spreads a wave over a region, and Hunt reaches only players within 60 m of the wave centre, because the game drops a target beyond about 86 m or out of sight (docs/features/SIEGES.md › S1 march spike). The job: a Region location (D1, D4), a region lock (D2, D5) and a region hunt that closes the distance by hidden jumps and then hands over to the game (D3, D7, D8). With the existing locations these give modes 1, 3 and 4 (D10, D24); mode 2 stays swarm's arena (D11); mode 5 is apocalypse's, which reuses this child's mover (design §9 D49). It extends SpawnWaves and coexists with every 0.10.0 event, which runs unchanged without the new keys (D11).

## Use cases
### Typical
The admin runs `.nyar template use spider-hunting-grounds`, `.nyar event enable spider-hunting-grounds` and `.nyar event start spider-hunting-grounds`. Spiders appear in groups of up to five at spots spread over Dunley Farmlands (D4). A player riding through the region 250 m from a group is the nearest eligible player: while nobody is within 100 m of the spiders they jump closer, 25 m at a time, unseen, and the last jump puts them 50-60 m from the player, where the game's own aggro takes over and they attack (D7, D8). The player flees across the border; the spiders chase, are brought back just inside within about 10 s and forget the player (D5). A player standing in their own castle is never targeted (D7). After 30 minutes the event ends and its units despawn (D12).

### Minimal stretch
- A Region location with one wave of one unit and no lock: one group of one at one spot in the region; it stays where it spawned and the event ends at its duration (D1, D4).
- A lock on a Point event with no behaviour: units stay where they are and are returned only if pushed or drawn out (D2, D5).
- RegionHunt with nobody in the regions: no target, no jump, no seed; the units stand at their spots (D7).
- Used once: the template stays disabled, every record dies with the event, nothing is written to disk (D12, D13, D16).

### Maximal stretch
- Volume: a Region wave is clamped once by MaxUnitsPerWave (at most 50) and the free MaxTrackedUnits slots (default 150), then split into groups of at most 5, so at most 10 groups a wave (D4); the lock reads each live unit of a locked event once per 5 s (D5); at most 20 units jump per check across all events (D8); a fourth concurrent event is refused by MaxConcurrentEvents (default 3), as today; D28 measures the tick.
- Abuse: a player can stand in claimed territory inside the region and is never targeted (D7); a player can stand just outside the border and shoot in, and the units do not follow out (D5); a player can try to bait jumps by standing 99 m away, and no unit jumps while any player is within 100 m (D8); a group of players spread over the region splits the groups by nearest target (D7).
- Repeated use: one instance per definition, as 0.8.0 (Epic Business rules 8); an event stopped and restarted inside its grace never moves the old instance's units (D5, D8, wave-sets A3).

## Business rules
1. **Region location** (D1, D4, S-12): each wave is clamped once, then split into groups of at most 5 units; each group's spot is drawn uniformly by area over the scope regions, inside a scope region, outside claimed territory unless allowTerritory, at least 30 m from the wave's other spots, on a free grounded tile whose height level is found at the spot; the group then spawns as any wave around its centre within action.radius. No player position places a Region wave.
2. **Region lock** (D2, D5, S-8, S-9): every 5 s (HuntPlan.IntervalSeconds, the existing behaviour cadence, as swarm's arena) a unit of the running instance standing in no scope region and more than 10 m from its last inside spot is returned within 10 m of that spot, inside a scope region, and drops its entries on players outside; while locked, no seed is written on a player outside the regions. The 10 m slack lets a unit finish a swing at the border, as swarm's S-13.
3. **Region hunt target** (D7, S-14, S-16): per group, the eligible player nearest to the group's mean position among those inside the scope regions, ties by key, chosen again each check. Eligible is Hunt's rule (online, alive, usable position, not in claimed territory, not in PvP combat).
4. **Hidden jumps** (D8, S-10, S-11, S-17): beyond 60 m a unit jumps toward its target only while no online player is within 100 m of it; at most 25 m a jump, the last one landing 50-60 m from the target; the landing free and grounded, inside a scope region, outside claimed territory and 50 m or more from every player; at most 20 jumps per check. Within 60 m the existing Hunt seeding hands the unit to the game's own pathing and aggro. Between 60 and 100 m nothing moves the unit: an engaged unit follows the game's chase, which holds to about 86 m with line of sight (SIEGES.md › S1); a unit that lost its target waits until its target is beyond 100 m again or within 60 m (S-17).
5. **Modes** (S-1): mode 1 is a Region location with the lock and RegionHunt (the template, D16); mode 2 is swarm's arena, unchanged and never combined with the lock (D2, D11); mode 3 is an AroundPlayer location with a regional scope, whose pick takes only players inside (D10), plus the lock; mode 4 is a Point location with the lock and RegionHunt (D24); mode 5 is apocalypse's (Out of scope).
6. **Precedence** (4.4): the caps (MaxUnitsPerWave, MaxTrackedUnits, MaxConcurrentEvents) beat the spread, the lock and the hunt (D4, D8); the kill switch beats everything (a purge ends the event and its lock and hunt with it, D12); validation beats chat (D14); the lock and the mover never move a unit this instance did not spawn (D5, D8); claimed territory beats the hunt (a player there is never a target and no jump lands there, D7, D8); the lock beats the hunt (a target is inside the regions by definition, and a seed on a player outside is never written, D5). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
7. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every unit of the running instance" is SpawnTracker's ledger filtered by event id and instance start (wave-sets A3), so `.nyar spawn` units and an earlier instance's units are outside it (D5, D8); "every group" is WaveAction's groups of the instance, one per Point or Admin wave, one per AroundPlayer group, one per Region spot (D7); "every end path" is the seven of D12; "every eligible player" is the online players PlayerQuery reads, filtered by Hunt's rule and the scope (D7); "every online player" in the 100 m and 50 m rules is every connected character PlayerQuery reads, eligible or not, so a dead, PvP or in-territory player still blocks a jump (D8); "every reader of the three values" is Validation, CommandArgs, DefinitionEditor, Authoring and EventAdmin, WaveAction's Region groups, the lock phase, HuntAction's seed filter and the hunt phase, each with an item (D1, D2, D3, D14, D15, D4, D5, D7, D8); "every location the lock applies to" is the four siblings below, each with its own D5 fails-when.
- whole: none — no list of parts here adds up to a total; the groups of a Region wave split one clamped wave, and the clamp is D4's own fails-when
- siblings: Point location, Admin location, AroundPlayer location, Region location

## Performance
- **13.1** Budget: the existing tick budget, 5 ms average at the default caps (automation D21). Recon measurement: swarm Session 1's timing span (docs/dod/swarm.md Log, D19 pass) held 10/10 windows under 5 ms with 24 tracked and its arena phase read "arena 0.1 ms", a position read per live unit every 5 s, which the lock repeats with one region lookup per unit (a box test, then a polygon test over 10 polygons; RegionTests Cost). The hunt's hot path is one PlayerQuery read per check (shared with Hunt), one target per group, and at most 20 landing checks per check inside the walk budget of 2500 calls a tick (WalkBudget.PerTick). - **13.2** Bounds: groups of at most 5 units, so at most 10 groups a wave at the ceiling of 50 units per wave (D4; the excluded case is one giant group at one spot, which is a Point location's job); spots 30 m apart with a bounded number of tries per group (S-12; the excluded case is a region too small for the wave's spots, whose groups are then not spawned); at most 20 jumps per check across all events (D8; the case is MaxTrackedUnits 150 jumping in one tick, and the excluded case is a large horde all closing in at once, which waits a few checks); a jump of at most 25 m (S-10; the excluded case is a group crossing a region in one tick); MaxConcurrentEvents bounds the phases' events, as today.
