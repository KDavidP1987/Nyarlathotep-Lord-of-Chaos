# DoD: Revenge — per-player faction heat, private warnings, a faction alert and ambush tiers around the player

**Size:** L. A schema change (a new trigger type `Revenge` in events.json) across several modules:
- **Logic:** Model, Validation, PlayerTriggers (the heat beside FactionKills' KillWindows), Engine (TriggerRouter, the focus-only hold), Spawning (PlayerPick), Messages, EventAdmin, CommandArgs, Authoring, ApiLines, Hooks, Limits, and a new Logic/Revenge.cs.
- **Services:** TriggerBus (the heat feed and the 5 s scan), WaveAction (the focus-only pick), EventRuntime (the end paths, the admin start's target), Announcer (the faction alert and the private lines), HealthMonitor; Patches/DeathEventPatch.cs reads one more fact (the V Blood flag) inside its existing guard.
- **Resources:** templates.json. **Tools:** tools/rollback-drill.ps1, tools/preflight-checks.json, tools/vrclient/scenarios/revenge*.vrs.
No new external dependency and no new cfg key.
**Planned:** interactively, drafted by a planning agent on 2026-10-11; the owner settled the tier model, concurrency and the alert wording on 2026-10-11 (design §9 D43: the tiers are the revenge's own waves, one revenge per definition and per player at a time, the alert names the faction only, heat in memory). The Halloween track's process applies (design §9 D34: one review round, two at most; Codex once on the child's diff; vrclient scenarios as the in-game evidence; the owner tests look and feel and anything needing a second player).
**Request:** Epic Child constraints › Halloween track: "**revenge** — per-player heat per faction from kills and V Blood kills within a window, private warnings to that player at rising thresholds, a faction alert when revenge starts, and growing ambush tiers around the player only, never in claimed territory (D36)." Design §9 D36: "Ambush near the player only: growing hunt squads around the player, never in claimed territory. A raid party on the player's castle belongs to sieges." D35: scoreboard only.

## Purpose & typical use
A server admin wants the world to push back: a vampire who slaughters a bandit camp, then another, then kills Rufus, should feel the bandits gather against them, hear it coming in private, and then be hunted by squads that grow until they are beaten. The rest of the server hears only that the bandits seek revenge. "Make the factions take revenge on players who farm them" is the sentence; the owner's players asked for "a revenge or ambush system" (Epic A35).

The job: per-player, per-faction heat from kills and V Blood kills within a window; private warnings at rising thresholds; a faction alert when revenge starts; growing ambush tiers around the player only, never in claimed territory (design §9 D36). It extends automation's FactionKills machinery (the kill read, the per-player counters, the player-action start and its focus) and reuses event-spawns' AroundPlayer and Hunt and wave-sets' waveList for the tiers and the scoreboard. It coexists with FactionKills, which stays exactly as it is. A raid on the player's castle is sieges', not this child's (D36).

## Use cases
### Typical
The admin uses `bandit-revenge`, enables it and turns the spawns pillar on. A player clears two bandit camps: after 10 points the player alone reads "The Bandits have noticed your killing.", after 20 "The Bandits are gathering against you." At 30 points, out on the road, the server reads "The Bandits swear revenge on a vampire." and the player "The Bandits come for you." Four bandits hunt the player from 20-40 m; when they die, six more come, then eight with a tough leader. The last one falls: "all waves defeated", the player reads "The Bandits' revenge is spent.", and the scoreboard shows who fought. For 30 minutes bandit kills add no heat for that player.

### Minimal stretch
- `warnAt` empty: no warning, the alert and the start line still come (D1, D4, D7).
- `vbloodHeat` 0: V Blood kills count nothing (D3).
- `playerCooldownMinutes` 0: heat counts again right after the start (D5).
- A units-form action with one wave: one squad, the event ends at its duration (D2, D6).
- Used once and never again: the template stays disabled; heat rows die with the definition's startability or the process; nothing is written to disk (D3, D9, D14).

### Maximal stretch
- Volume: 40 players killing bandits at once hold at most 200 heat rows per definition and 2000 in all, the least recently updated dropped (D3); each death costs one rule check per startable Revenge definition, inside the read FactionKills already does (D10, D23); one revenge per definition runs at a time and every unit stays under MaxUnitsPerWave, MaxTrackedUnits and MaxConcurrentEvents (D5, Epic Business rules 8).
- Abuse: a player can hide in their castle while due; the revenge waits outside (held, D5) until the heat leaves the window, and a running revenge's waves wait outside (D6); a player can log out, and the event's duration ends it; a player can farm the ambush units, which add no heat (ours, D3) and count on the scoreboard only (D35); a group can split the kills, and each member's heat counts alone (S-8).
- Repeated use: a second player who reaches `heat` while the definition runs is held "busy" and starts after it ends, if still hot (D5); a player hot for two definitions gets the second after the first ends ("target busy", D5); the same player cannot be re-targeted within the cooldown (D5).

## Business rules
1. **Heat** (D3): per definition and player, the sum of the points of counted kills within the last windowSeconds; a counted kill gives 1 point, a V Blood kill vbloodHeat; a point leaves windowSeconds after its kill (the decay, S-9); nothing is added during the cooldown.
2. **Warnings** (D4): warning k when the heat rises to or past warnAt[k]; once per climb, the highest only when one kill passes two; re-armed below the threshold.
3. **Due and start** (D5, D13): heat at or past `heat` makes the player due; a due player starts when eligible and when neither the definition nor the player is busy; the start resets the heat and starts the cooldown; a conditions refusal resets the heat only. An admin start targets the admin and touches no heat.
4. **Tiers** (D6; design §9 D43): the tiers are the waves of the revenge's own SpawnWaves action: a waveList grows them per wave and starts each when the previous is cleared or after its afterSeconds (design §9 D40); the instance ends at all waves defeated (D41) or its duration.
5. **Where** (D6, D36, design §9 D46): every tier around the target only, and every squad's Hunt seeds the target only; a companion who attacks a squad is fought through the game's own aggro, never seeded; a tier waits while the target is not eligible; never a centre, ring point or hunt target in claimed territory.
6. **Who hears what** (D4, D7, D8; design §9 D43, D47): the target alone gets the warnings, the start line and the end line; the server gets the alert, which names no player and no place (Epic D16); the target stays anonymous while the revenge runs; the end-of-event scoreboard follows wave-sets D38 and names its top 3, the recorded exception (design §9 D47).
7. **Precedence** (4.4): the kill switch beats everything (a purge ends the instance, empties all heat, and sends no line, D8); the caps beat revenge (a clamped tier is still one tier); one instance per definition beats a second target (held "busy", D5, design §9 D43); claimed territory beats the ambush (held, D6); validation beats chat (D11). The owner decides an exception, through design §9.
8. **Memory only** (D9; S-11): heat, due players, warning states and cooldowns are lost on every boot; the running instance is cancelled at boot as every event (Epic S-11).
9. **"Every" sets** (4.5): "every death read" is the one DeathEvent postfix (Patches/DeathEventPatch.cs), whose FactionKills read the heat shares (D10); "every player-facing revenge line" is the four builders of D9 (RevengeWarning, RevengeStart, RevengeEnd, RevengeAlertPool), enumerated by PrivacyTests' reflection over Logic/Messages; "every end path" of a revenge instance is the six of D8; "every wave pick" is Services/WaveAction.cs Pick through Logic PlayerPick (the only AroundPlayer pick) and Hunt's target choice, Logic/Spawning.cs HuntPlan.Targets (who a squad comes for, D6, design §9 D46); "every reader of a trigger type's fields" is the files that switch on TriggerType (recon: Logic/Validation.cs, CommandArgs.cs, EventAdmin.cs, ApiLines.cs, Hooks.cs, Engine.cs TriggerRouter, PlayerTriggers.cs), and a new type without a branch falls to their default, which D1, D11, D12 and D15's tests catch.
- whole: none — heat is a per-player count compared with thresholds; nothing it holds adds up to a total a user reconciles (the scoreboard's totals are wave-sets D8's)
- siblings: natural end, all waves defeated, admin stop, purge, restart, pillar off

### States
- **7.1** Empty: no Revenge definition startable → no death is read for it and no row exists (D10); first run: the first counted kill makes a row (D3); partial: a held player stays due (D5), a held wave waits (D6); error: a refused definition is disabled with its reason (D1, D2), a failing read holds its health entry (D10); `.nyar event info` shows the counts (D12).
- **7.2** Concurrent use: two players due on one definition → the earlier starts, the other held "busy" (D5); one player due on two definitions → the earlier starts, the other held "target busy" (D5); two kills of one player in one frame are counted in DeathEvent order on the main thread (no locks, foundation's serialisation); a scan and a death in one tick never start twice (the start is synchronous and the second finds the definition active, automation 7.2). The planner (Claude) and the build tooling act on the dev server only between sessions, wrapped by the snapshot (D26); the orchestrating session owns the server and client.
- **7.3** Cancel and re-entry: every end path of D8 (natural end, all waves defeated, admin stop, purge, restart, pillar off) and what it leaves; a definition edited while running keeps its definition (Engine), and the heat rows of an edited definition keep counting under the new trigger's window from the reload; a definition disabled loses its rows (D3); a player who relogs keeps their rows (platform id); a stale due player whose heat left the window is no longer due (D5).

## Performance
- **13.1** Budget: the existing tick budget, 5 ms average at the default caps (automation D21). Hot paths: the death postfix (one KillRule check and one row update per startable Revenge definition per death, inside the FactionKills read) and the 5 s scan (one pass over the due players, at most the rows, with the player read automation already does). - **13.2** Bounds: heat 3-500 and windowSeconds 60-3600 (D1; 60 s is the shortest window a camp clear spans, 3600 s matches FactionKills' ceiling); 200 heat rows a definition and 2000 in all (TriggerLimits, automation's bound: a separate bound with the same constants, so heat rows and FactionKills' counters never evict each other and the two together hold at most 4000; the excluded case is a 201st concurrent killer of one faction, whose oldest-row neighbour loses heat); warnAt at most 3 (three warnings keep chat calm); one running revenge per definition (Epic Business rules 8) and per player (D5).
