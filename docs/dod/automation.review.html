4. Business rules & invariants

What the plan says under “Business rules”

  1. **Interval (D1, D2):** the next start is drawn uniformly in whole seconds between minMinutes and maxMinutes after the moment the event was last seen inactive: its end, a refused start, the first poll after an enable or a reload, or the boot. It is kept in state.json and survives a restart when still in the future; one at or before the boot is redrawn, so downtime is never replayed (Epic Business rules 6). While an instance runs there is no next start. A purge's cooldown refuses a due start, which redraws (D22). A server clock stepped forward makes every passed next due at once: each fires once, bounded by MaxConcurrentEvents, and each refused one redraws (D2, D14).
  2. **Fan-out (D4, D5, D6):** - Only with an AroundPlayer location. - Each wave picks up to maxInstances eligible players (event-spawns D16's rule), each at least minSpacing metres from every one already picked, the focus player first. - Each group is rolled on its own. The whole wave, all groups together, is clamped to MaxUnitsPerWave and the free MaxTrackedUnits, as any wave (S-4); the allowed units are dealt round robin to the groups in pick order. - A claimed centre skips only its group. - Each group carries its own HuntTag and hunts by Hunt's rule (the nearest eligible player within Hunt range of its units), so a group engages its own centre player first unless another stands nearer. - It is one instance with one wave count: the one-instance rule is unchanged.
  3. **RegionEntered (D8, D9):** an entry is a move from a region outside the trigger's scope to one inside it, seen between two 5 s scans; a transit through a region shorter than one scan (bat form across a corner) is not an entry, by design; an unmapped gap counts as outside every scope; a first sighting is not an entry; the per-player cooldown counts from the last entry that reached a start attempt and, keyed on the platform id, outlives a relog.
  4. **FactionKills (D10, D11):** a kill counts when the killer is a player (or a player's familiar, summon or projectile), the victim is of a listed faction, and the victim is neither our unit, a minion nor the killer; with a scope, the victim stands in it. N kills within W seconds fire, per player or, with shared, for all players together; the counter then empties.
  5. **Focus (D13):** a player-action start remembers the triggering player for its AroundPlayer waves, in memory only; if that player is not eligible, any eligible player is picked.
  6. **One start path (D14):** every new trigger goes through TriggerRouter.Candidates and EventRuntime.StartEvent, so Epic Business rules 1's precedence (kill switch > General.Enabled > pillar switch > caps > the definition) and conditions apply as for every trigger.
  7. **Numbers stated twice:** the ranges live in Logic/Validation.cs and are quoted here and in the READMEs; Validation.cs is authoritative (Epic Business rules 10). The default caps are in Logic/Limits.cs.
  8. **Precedence between the new rules (4.4):** - A trigger reaching an active event is dropped before the conditions (D14). - The conditions come before the caps; the caps (MaxUnitsPerWave, MaxTrackedUnits) bound the whole fanned-out wave, and maxInstances and minSpacing only choose the groups inside that bound (S-4). - The per-player cooldown is checked before the dedupe. - The owner decides exceptions by editing the definition or the cfg caps; no rule has an exception in code.
  9. **"Every X" sets (4.5):** - "Every Interval definition" is TriggerRouter.Candidates(set, Interval); IntervalClock.Load prunes state.json against it at boot, and each poll prunes an id that has left it (D2). - "Every end path" needs no hook: the clock sees the event inactive on the next poll, whatever ended it (D2, D22). - "Every player" of a scan is PlayerQuery.Read's rows: connected users with a readable character. A player the read leaves out makes no entry and loses its region row (D9, D15). - "Every kill" is a DeathEvent row of DeathEventListenerSystem, the same source as VBloodKilled (DEV_REMINDERS #26). A unit destroyed without dying (despawn, purge) raises none and counts nothing, which is intended (D11). - "Every group" of a wave goes through one clamp (D6). - "Every new line" is listed in D19 and D27. - "Every path" this child writes is in Rollout › Paths walked (D25).

D2 Interval clock

Logic/Schedule.cs `IntervalClock.Poll(minMinutes, maxMinutes, next, active, utcNow, rng)` returns whether to fire and the new next: while the event is active the next is removed; with no next it draws utcNow plus a uniform whole number of seconds in minMinutes×60..maxMinutes×60 and does not fire; a next later than utcNow + maxMinutes (a shortened definition) is redrawn; a next at or before utcNow fires once and is removed, so after a refused start or any end path (natural end, stop, fault cancel, purge) the next poll with the event inactive draws from that moment; `IntervalClock.Load(stored, set, bootUtc, rng)` keeps only ids of startable Interval definitions and redraws a next at or before bootUtc, so downtime is never replayed (Epic Business rules 6); Services/TriggerBus.cs writes every change to state.json `NextInterval` (event id → UTC) and marks it dirty; a failed flush keeps the value in memory (Persistence's streak)
